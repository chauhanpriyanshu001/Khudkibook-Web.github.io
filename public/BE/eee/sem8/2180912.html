Condition Monitoring Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Condition Monitoring (subject code 2180912) as prescribed for EEE · Semester 8 divides the course into 6 units, covering 79 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

4 lecture hours

  • different maintenance strategies like Breakdown maintenance

  • dielectric absorption ratio
  • Insulation ageing mechanisms
  • Insulation failure modes
  • Definition of terms
  • Concept of condition monitoring of electrical equipments
  • Overview of Advanced tools and techniques of condition monitoring
  • Condition monitoring by thermography

  • Introduction and Basic Philosophy
  • Advantages and Disadvantages of DFR measurement in Time and Frequency domain
