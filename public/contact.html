How We Can Help

  • Book Requests: If your textbook isn't listed yet, request it and we'll try to add the English and Gujarati medium PDFs.
  • Paper Updates: Previous year papers are added after every GTU exam session — tell us if your session is missing.
  • Syllabus Corrections: Found an outdated syllabus? Send us the subject code and the year of your scheme.
  • App Help: For Android app issues, mention your device and the Khudkibook app version.

How long do replies take?

We answer most messages within 24–48 hours. Book requests are collected weekly and published in batches.

Are the books really free?

Yes. Everything on Khudkibook — books, papers, and syllabus — is completely free for students. We never ask for payment or personal details to download.
