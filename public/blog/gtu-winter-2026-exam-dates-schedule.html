How To Read The Schedule

  • Find your programme in the attached PDF, then note the theory paper dates and the date of the viva-voce / practical.
  • Check whether the schedule is tentative — GTU often revises dates when colleges cannot provide halls or faculty, and a revised circular supersedes the original.
  • Note the last date for downloading the hall ticket, which comes separately.
Work backwards from the first paper date, not from today. If you have four subjects and three weeks, plan two subjects a week and leave the last week for revision only.
