## 5.2. Samples for Practice

### 5.2.1. Welcome Speech

#### **Definition:**
A welcome speech is a formal or informal address given to greet and extend a warm welcome to a person or group. It is used in various settings such as meetings, conferences, or social gatherings.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Welcoming a distinguished guest at a seminar.
> >
> > **Speech:**
> >
> > **[Introduction]**
> >
> > "Ladies and gentlemen, esteemed professors, and distinguished guests, good morning."
> >
> > **[Expression of Greeting and Welcome]**
> >
> > "I would like to extend a heartfelt welcome to all of you on this beautiful morning. It is a pleasure to see such a distinguished group of experts and scholars gathered here today."
> >
> > **[Acknowledgment of the Guest]**
> >
> > "Let me specifically welcome our esteemed guest, Professor Dr. XYZ, who has traveled from abroad to share his invaluable insights with us. Your presence is a testament to the importance of this event."
> >
> > **[Purpose and Significance]**
> >
> > "Today, we come together to discuss the future of sustainable energy, a topic that is of great importance and relevance. Your guidance and expertise will undoubtedly enrich our understanding and contribute to the progress of this field."
> >
> > **[Closing]**
> >
> > "Once again, welcome to all of you. Let us make this seminar a success and a memorable experience. Thank you for your attention."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Ensure that your pronunciation is clear and your intonation is appropriate for the formal setting.

### 5.2.2. Farewell Speech

#### **Definition:**
A farewell speech is a formal or informal address given to bid farewell to a person or group. It is used in various contexts such as retirement, graduation, or the end of a long-standing relationship.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Farewell party for a retiring faculty member.
> >
> > **Speech:**
> >
> > **[Introduction]**
> >
> > "Dear colleagues, friends, and students, it is with a bittersweet feeling that I stand before you today."
> >
> > **[Expression of Gratitude]**
> >
> > "First and foremost, I would like to extend my heartfelt gratitude to Professor Dr. ABC, who has been a beacon of knowledge and inspiration for us all. Your contributions to our institution have been immense."
> >
> > **[Recollection of the Tenure]**
> >
> > "Over the past 25 years, you have guided countless students, published groundbreaking research, and mentored future leaders. Your dedication and commitment have set a high standard for us all."
> >
> > **[Acknowledgment of Achievements]**
> >
> > "Your scholarly work and pioneering research have brought honor to our institution. Your dedication to teaching and mentorship has shaped the lives of many, making a positive impact on our community."
> >
> > **[Future and Closing]**
> >
> > "As you embark on this new journey, we wish you the very best. May your days be filled with joy, prosperity, and fulfillment. We will always cherish the memories we have shared."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a warm and appreciative tone throughout the speech to convey your sincerity and gratitude.

### 5.2.3. Introducing Oneself and Another

#### **Definition:**
Introducing oneself involves presenting oneself to others, often in a brief and concise manner. Introducing another person involves presenting someone else to a group or individual.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Introducing a colleague at a company meeting.
> >
> > **Self-Introduction:**
> >
> > "Good morning, everyone. I would like to introduce myself. My name is XYZ, and I joined the company last month. I specialize in software development and am excited to contribute to our projects."
> >
> > **Introducing Another Person:**
> >
> > "Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to introduce our new colleague, Dr. ABC. Dr. ABC has over 15 years of experience in the field of data science and is here to lead our analytics team. Please join me in giving her a warm welcome."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Ensure that your pronunciation is clear and your intonation conveys confidence and professionalism.

### 5.2.4. Discussing Weather

#### **Definition:**
Discussing weather involves describing the current or forecasted conditions, which can be relevant in various contexts such as travel, outdoor activities, or weather briefings.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Briefing about the weather for a weekend trip.
> >
> > **Weather Report:**
> >
> > "Good evening, everyone. Today, the weather forecast for the weekend is looking promising. We can expect clear skies and mild temperatures. The high is expected to reach 25 degrees Celsius, with a low of 18 degrees at night. The chance of precipitation is minimal, so you can enjoy the outdoors without worrying about rain."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a clear and confident tone to convey accurate and relevant information.

### 5.2.5. Disposal of E-Waste

#### **Definition:**
E-waste disposal involves the responsible and sustainable management of electronic waste, which is important for environmental protection.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Giving a speech on the importance of e-waste management.
> >
> > **Speech:**
> >
> > "Good morning, everyone. Today, I would like to discuss the critical issue of e-waste management. With the rapid advancement of technology, the generation of electronic waste is on the rise. It is our responsibility to ensure that this waste is disposed of in an environmentally friendly manner. Proper disposal not only protects our environment but also conserves resources."
> >
> > **[Importance and Impact]**
> >
> > "The improper disposal of e-waste can lead to severe environmental pollution, including toxic substances leaching into soil and water sources. By recycling and properly disposing of e-waste, we can reduce these risks and promote sustainable practices."
> >
> > **[Solutions and Actions]**
> >
> > "To address this issue, we should encourage the use of e-waste recycling services, support the development of recycling infrastructure, and educate the public about the importance of responsible disposal. Together, we can make a significant difference."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a serious and informative tone to emphasize the importance of the topic.

### 5.2.6. Environmental Protection through Non-Use of Plastic

#### **Definition:**
Non-use of plastic is a strategy to reduce plastic waste and promote environmental sustainability.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Speech on reducing plastic usage.
> >
> > **Speech:**
> >
> > "Ladies and gentlemen, today, I would like to discuss the importance of reducing our plastic usage. Plastic pollution is a significant environmental issue, affecting our oceans, wildlife, and overall ecosystem. By reducing our reliance on single-use plastics, we can make a substantial difference."
> >
> > **[Current Situation]**
> >
> > "Every year, millions of tons of plastic end up in our oceans, harming marine life and ecosystems. This not only poses a threat to biodiversity but also affects human health through contaminated seafood."
> >
> > **[Solutions and Actions]**
> >
> > "To address this, we should promote the use of alternatives such as biodegradable materials, reusable containers, and packaging. Encouraging businesses and individuals to adopt these practices can significantly reduce plastic waste."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a persuasive and passionate tone to encourage action.

### 5.2.7. Reduction of Noise Pollution by Vehicles

#### **Definition:**
Noise pollution from vehicles is a significant environmental issue that can be mitigated through various measures.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Speech on reducing vehicle noise.
> >
> > **Speech:**
> >
> > "Good evening, everyone. Today, I would like to discuss the issue of noise pollution from vehicles. Excessive noise from vehicles can have serious impacts on human health and the environment. It can lead to sleep disturbances, stress, and even hearing loss."
> >
> > **[Current Situation]**
> >
> > "In densely populated areas, the constant honking and engine noise can create a noisy environment that is detrimental to quality of life. This noise can also contribute to increased stress levels and decreased productivity."
> >
> > **[Solutions and Actions]**
> >
> > "To reduce this noise, we should encourage the use of quieter vehicles, implement noise barriers, and promote the use of public transportation. Additionally, enforcing stricter noise regulations and penalties for excessive noise can help in achieving a quieter environment."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a serious and informative tone to highlight the urgency of the issue.

### 5.2.8. Conversation with the Cashier

#### **Definition:**
A conversation with a cashier involves interacting with a financial service provider in a retail or banking setting.

> **Example:**
>
> > **Example:**  
> >
> > **Situation:** Purchasing tickets at a college auditorium.
> >
> > **Conversation:**
> >
> > "Excuse me, I would like to buy some tickets for the upcoming seminar."
> >
> > **[Response from Cashier]**
> >
> > "Of course, we have tickets available for both the morning and afternoon sessions. Could you please confirm the number of tickets you require?"
> >
> > **[Purchasing the Tickets]**
> >
> > "I would like to buy two tickets for the morning session. Can you tell me the price?"
> >
> > **[Cashier's Response]**
> >
> > "Sure, each morning ticket costs 500 rupees. The total for two tickets is 1000 rupees."
> >
> > **[Payment]**
> >
> > "Thank you, I will pay with a credit card. Here are my details: [credit card number] and the expiration date is [date]."
> >
> > **[Cashier's Confirmation]**
> >
> > "Thank you, the transaction is complete. Here are your tickets. Enjoy the seminar."
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Ensure your pronunciation is clear and your tone is polite and professional.

### 5.2.9. Telephonic Conversations (Formal and Informal)

Telephonic conversations involve communicating over the phone, which can be formal or informal depending on the context.

> >

> > "Hello, it's XYZ. How are you doing today?"
> >
> > **[Response from Friend]**
> >
> > "Hello, it's ABC. I'm doing well, thanks for asking. How about you?"
> >
> > **[Discussion]**
> >
> > "I'm doing great, thanks. I just wanted to catch up and see what you've been up to."
> >
> > **[Formal Conversation with a Colleague]**
> >
> > "Hello, it's XYZ. I'm calling to discuss the upcoming project with you."
> >
> > **[Response from Colleague]**
> >
> > "Hello, it's ABC. Sure, what do you need to discuss?"
> >
> > **[Discussion]**
> >
> > "We need to finalize the project timeline and allocate the resources. Can we meet tomorrow to discuss the details?"
> >
> > **[Pronunciation and Intonation]**
> >
> > Use a clear and polite tone in both informal and formal conversations.
