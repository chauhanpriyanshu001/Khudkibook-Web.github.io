Manufacturing Engineering - III Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Manufacturing Engineering - III (subject code DI05019011) as prescribed for Mechatronics · Semester 5 divides the course into 5 units, covering 50 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Production Tooling

10 lecture hours

  • Fixtures & Press Tools Principles of Location and Clamping 1.1 Need for jigs and fixtures 1.2 Degrees of freedom and 3-2-1 location principle 1.3 Locator (pin
  • button, V, nest locator) and Clamps (strap and C-clamp) Jigs: 1.4 Types
  • construction and applications (plate, box, leaf and channel jig) 1.5 Drill bushes: Types (fixed and renewable) and applications Fixtures: 1.5 Milling
  • welding and assembly fixtures
  • Press Tools: 1.6 Construction
  • working and application of press tool 1.7 Sheet metal operations (shearing
  • piercing and bending) 1.8 Types of dies: simple
  • compound and progressive

9 lecture hours

  • tool geometry
  • advantage and limitation) 2.6 Gear Finishing Processes (need and types) 2.7 Gear shaving and grinding (principle
  • achievable accuracy) Thread Production Processes 2.8 Thread terminology and application 2.9 Thread cutting • By single-point cutting tool • By taps and dies 2.10 Thread rolling • By flat-die • By cylindrical-die

Unit 3 Non-conventional and Advanced Methods of Machining

10 lecture hours

  • 3.1 Need of non-conventional machining and comparison between conventional & non-conventional machining methods. 3.2 Classification
  • working principles

  • deposition) 3.7 Applications of MEMS in engineering and industry Program Name: Diploma Engineering Level: Diploma Branch: Mechanical Engineering Subject Code: DI05019011 Subject Name: Manufacturing Engineering - III
