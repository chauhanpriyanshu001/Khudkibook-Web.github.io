Nanotechnology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Nanotechnology (subject code ME01072071) as prescribed for CE(A · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 41 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01072071)

Unit 1 Introduction

8 lecture hours

  • Introduction to nanotechnology
  • Classification of nanostructures
  • Nanoscale architecture
  • Chemical interactions at nanoscale, One, two and multidimensional structures
  • Semiconducting Nanomaterials
  • Types of carbon- based nanomaterials
  • Metal oxide nanomaterials
  • Optical Properties of Nanomaterials
  • Magnetic Properties of Nanomaterials

Unit 2 Approaches to Synthesis of Nanoscale Materials Top down

12 lecture hours

  • Bottom-up approach Bottom-up vs. topdown fabrication
  • Top-down: Atomization
  • Sol gel technique
  • Arc discharge
  • Laser ablation
  • RF sputtering
  • Bottom-up: Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD)
  • Metal Oxide Chemical Vapor Deposition (MOCVD)
  • Atomic layer deposition (ALD)
  • Molecular beam Molecular self- assembly
  • Ultrasound assisted
  • microwave assisted
  • micro- and nanoemulsion
  • Wet grinding method
  • Spray pyrolysis
  • Ultrasound assisted pyrolysis
  • atomization techniques
  • Surfactant based synthesis procedures

Unit 3 Characterization of Nanoscale Materials Types of molecular

7 lecture hours

  • modelling methods
  • chemistry analysis using X-ray imaging
  • Transmission Electron Microscopy
  • Scanning Electron Microscopy, SPM, AFM, STM, PSD, Zeta potential
  • DSC and TGA

Unit 4 Application in the field of chemical Nanocatalysts, Photocatalysts,

8 lecture hours

  • Nanomaterials for Adsorbent Application
  • Food and agricultural industries
  • pharmaceutical products
  • Nano composite polymers

Unit 5 Applications to Safety, Environment and Others Biosensors,

10 lecture hours

  • Nanostructured Materials for Sensing
  • Waste Water Treatment
  • Self-cleaning Nano Materials
  • Biological nanomaterials
  • Health and Environmental Impacts
