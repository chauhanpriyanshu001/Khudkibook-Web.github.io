Medical Regulatory Affairs Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Medical Regulatory Affairs (subject code 4360303) as prescribed for ELECTRICAL · Semester 6 divides the course into 5 units, covering 37 listed topics. Unit 1 Introduction to medical Regulatory Affairs

  • 1.1. Role of Regulatory Affairs in the Biomedical Industry
  • 1.2. Regulatory Agencies and their Functions : FDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration), EMA (European Medicines Agency), The Central Drugs Standard Control Organization(CDSCO), IPC, NPPA and Other global regulatory authorities
  • 1.3. Classification of Medical device a per FDA
  • 1.4. Classification of Medical device a per CDSCO
  • 1.5. Regulatory requirements for different device classes

Unit 2 Medical device safety and risk management

  • 2.1. Medical device safety and risk management
  • 2.2. Phases in life span of medical device 2.3Critical elements for regulatory attention
  • 2.3.1. product and use
  • 2.3.2. product representation
  • 2.4. Stages of regulatory control
  • 2.5. Common framework of medical device
  • 2.6. Regulatory tools and general requirements
  • 2.6.1. Product control
  • 2.7. GHTF(Global Harmonization Task Force) Medical Regulatory Affairs Course Code: 4360303 NITTTR Bhopal – GTU - COGC-2022 Curriculum

Unit 3 Standards

  • 3.1.1. Need of standards
  • 3.1.2. Voluntary and mandatory standards
  • 3.1.3. Standard Development process
  • 3.1.4. National and International Standard system
  • 3.1.5. Identifications of standards
  • 3.1.6. Current trends in use of standards in medical device regulations
  • 3.1.7. BIS- Bureau of Indian Standards
  • 3.2. Environmental Considerations in medical device regulations

Unit 4 Regulation and standards in medical devices for EU European Union

  • 4.1. Harmonized standards and various bodies in EU
  • 4.2. risk based classification of medical device in EU
  • 4.3. CE mark and requirement of medical device for CE mark 4.4 procedure to obtain CE mark
  • 4.5. labelling process
  • 4.6. technical documentation and risk management system
  • 4.7. pre market and post market scenario of CE marked medical device

Unit 5 Regulation and standards in medical devices for India United states medical

  • 5.1. laws governing medical device and governing bodies
  • 5.2. risk based classification of medical device
  • 5.3. role of distributors and subsidiary bodies
  • 5.4. product registration process and quality system regulations
  • 5.5. labelling and technical requirements
  • 5.6. clinical trails
  • 5.7. commercial aspects and regulations on price
  • 5.8. Upcoming medical regulations in India Medical Regulatory Affairs Course Code: 4360303 NITTTR Bhopal – GTU - COGC-2022 Curriculum
