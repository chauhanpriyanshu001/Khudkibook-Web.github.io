Analog Circuit Design Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Analog Circuit Design (subject code 2141002) as prescribed for ETC · Semester 4 divides the course into 10 units, covering 88 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2141002)

Unit 1 The Transistor at High Frequencies

7 lecture hours

  • Hybrid–pi CE transistor model
  • Hybrid–pi conductances
  • Hybrid–pi capacitances
  • Validity of hybrid–pi model
  • Variation of hybrid–pi parameters
  • CE short-circuit current gain
  • Current gain with resistive load
  • Single-stage CE transistor amplifier response
  • Gain-bandwidth product
  • Emitter follower at high frequencies

Unit 2 Oscillators

5 lecture hours

  • Sinusoidal oscillators
  • Phase-shift oscillator
  • Resonant circuit oscillators
  • A general form of oscillator circuit
  • Wien bridge oscillator
  • Crystal oscillators
  • Frequency stability

Unit 3 Operational Amplifiers

3 lecture hours

  • The basic operational amplifier
  • The differential amplifier
  • The emitter- coupled differential amplifier
  • Transfer characteristics of a differential amplifier

Unit 4 Introduction to Operational Amplifiers, Interpretation of Data Sheets and Characteristics of an Op-amp

3 lecture hours

  • Block Diagram representation of a typical op-amp
  • its equivalent circuits
  • types of ICs, Manufacturers’ designations and package types for ICs, Power supplies for ICs, Interpreting datasheet
  • Ideal op-amp
  • Equivalent circuit of an op-amp
  • Ideal voltage transfer curve
  • Open-loop op-amp configurations

Unit 5 An Op-amp with Negative Feedback

4 lecture hours

  • Voltage series feedback amplifier
  • Voltage shunt feedback amplifier
  • Differential Amplifier

Unit 6 Op-amp Offset Voltage

4 lecture hours

  • Input offset voltage
  • Input bias current
  • Input offset current
  • Total output offset voltage
  • Thermal Drift
  • Effect of variation in power supply voltages on offset voltage
  • Change in input offset voltage and input offset current with time
  • Other temperature and supply voltage sensitive parameters

Unit 7 General Linear Applications

8 lecture hours

  • DC and AC Amplifiers
  • AC amplifiers with single supply voltage
  • Peaking amplifier
  • Scaling and Averaging Amplifier
  • Instrumentation Amplifier – its block diagram along with applications
  • Differential input and differential output amplifier
  • Voltage-to-current converter with floating load (low voltage DC voltmeter
  • low voltage AC voltmeter
  • Diode match finder
  • Zener diode tester
  • Light emitting diode tester)
  • Current-to-voltage converter

Unit 8 Comparators and Converters

6 lecture hours

  • Zero crossing detector
  • Schmitt Trigger
  • Voltage limiters
  • Clipper and clampers
  • Absolute value output circuit
  • Peak detector
  • Sample and hold circuit
  • Precision rectifier – Half/Full Wave
  • Triangular and Saw tooth wave generator
  • Common mode configuration and common mode rejection ratio
  • Slew rate and its equations
  • Effect of slew rate in applications

Unit 9 Specialized ICs and its Applications

4 lecture hours

  • a. 555 Timer and its applications Block diagram
  • Monostable and Astable multivibrator
  • Applications as Frequency divider
  • Square wave generator b
  • Phase Locked Loops and its Applications: Block diagram and operation
  • Applications as Frequency Multiplier
  • Frequency Shift Keying c
  • Design of Power Supply: Simple op-amp voltage regulator
  • Three terminal voltage regulators
  • Fixed and adjustable voltage regulators (78XX
  • Heat sink
  • Dual power supply (LM320
  • Basic switching regulator and its characteristics

Unit 10 Op-amp based Filter Circuits

8 lecture hours

  • Classification of filters
  • Magnitude and frequency scaling
  • Magnitude and attenuation characteristics of ideal and practical filters
  • Design parameter Q and ω0
  • Biquad (Universal) filter design
  • Butterworth low- pass and high-pass filters – 1st and 2nd order circuits design
  • Butterworth pole location
  • Sallen and key circuit
  • Butterworth bandpass filters – frequency transformation
  • Deliyannis – friend Circuit
  • Chebyshev filter characteristics
  • Band reject filters
