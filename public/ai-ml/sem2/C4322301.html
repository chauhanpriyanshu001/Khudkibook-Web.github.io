Polymer Chemistry Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Polymer Chemistry (subject code C4322301) as prescribed for AI & ML · Semester 2 divides the course into 6 units, covering 41 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Polymerization techniques

  • 1.2. Bulk Polymerization
  • 1.3. Solution Polymerization
  • 1.4. Suspension Polymerization
  • 1.5. Emulsion Polymerization
  • 1.6. Melt Polycondensation
  • 1.7. Solution Polycondensation
  • 1.8. Interfacial Condensation
  • 1.9. Solid and Gas Phase Polymerization

Unit 2 Glass transition temperature

  • 2.2. State & phase A. Glassy state B. Visco-elastic state C. Visco-fluid state D. Solid phase E. Liquid phase
  • 2.3. State of Aggregation
  • 2.4. Orientation of polymer (IBM & EBM)
  • 2.5. Glass transition temperature and associated properties
  • 2.6. Factor Influencing Glass transition temperature
  • 2.7. Glass transition temperature of copolymers
  • 2.8. Glass transition temperature and Polymer Chemistry Course Code: C4322301 Molecular Weight
  • 2.9. Glass transition temperature and Melting Point
  • 2.10. Glass transition temperature and Plasticizer
  • 2.11. Importance of Glass transition temperature

Unit 3 Structure of polymer

  • 3.2. Polymer morphology
  • 3.3. Dispersity A. Mono Dispersity B. Poly Dispersity
  • 3.4. Molecular weight and its distribution A. Average weight concept B. Types of Molecular weight C. Degree of polymerization A. Significance of Molecular weight
  • 3.5. Microstructure based on A. Chemical structure B. Geometric structure
  • 3.6. Degree of crystallinity
  • 3.7. Crystalline and amorphous structure of polymer
  • 3.8. Polymer crystallization and crystallisability
  • 3.9. Factors affecting crystallisability
  • 3.10. Effect of crystallinity on polymer properties

Unit 4 Polymer degradation

  • 4.2. Types of degradation A. Chain-end B. Random
  • 4.3. Thermal Degradation
  • 4.4. Mechanical Degradation
  • 4.5. Ultrasonic wave Degradation
  • 4.6. Photo degradation
  • 4.7. Oxidative Degradation
  • 4.8. Hydrolytic Degradation
  • 4.9. Means to Reduce Polymer degradation
  • 4.10. Factor Affecting Polymer degradation

Unit 5 Polymer reactionsand polymer solutions

  • 5.6. Addition reactions
  • 5.7. Substitution reactions
  • 5.8. Cross-linked reactions
  • 5.9. Polymer dissolution A. dissolution process B. effect of MW on solubility Polymer Chemistry Course Code: C4322301
