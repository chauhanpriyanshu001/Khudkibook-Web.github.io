Power Electronics For Renewable Energy Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Power Electronics For Renewable Energy (subject code 3362401) as prescribed for AI & DS · Semester 6 divides the course into 5 units, covering 28 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3362401)

Unit 1 Renewable Energy Power Plants

  • 1.1. Renewable energy and environment.
  • 1.2. Types of Renewable Energy Sources
  • 1.3. Wave energy power plants
  • 1.4. Marine current power plants
  • 1.5. Ocean Thermal Energy Power Plants
  • 1.6. Geothermal energy power plants

Unit 2 Power Electronics in Solar PV Systems

  • 2.1. Photo Voltaic(PV): cell, module, array and panel
  • 2.2. Home solar PV system
  • 2.3. Components of a home solar system
  • 2.4. Types of batteries used in solar PV system
  • 2.5. Charge Controller
  • 2.6. Signal Conditioner
  • 2.8. Power Electronic Devices Used In a solar PV system.
  • 2.9. Power configuration for grid-connected PV systems: central, string and module inverters configuration.

Unit 3 Power

  • 3.1. Wind energy basics: wind requirement and in windy sites Power Electronics for Renewable Energy Course Code: 3362401 GTU/ NITTTR Bhopal/14 Gujarat State 3 Unit Major Learning Outcomes (In Cognitive Domain) Topics and Sub-topics Electronics in Large Wind Power Plants
  • 3.2. Aerodynamics of Wind power Plants: stall, active- stall and pitch control
  • 3.3. Geared wind power plants
  • 3.4. Direct drive wind power plants
  • 3.5. Semi-geared or hybrid wind power plants
  • 3.6. Type-A, Type-B, Type-C, Type-D, wind power plants
  • 3.7. Thyristors, IGBT, GTO, IGCT
  • 3.8. Power electronic circuits: Soft starters, Back-to-back converters, Multi-level converters

Unit 4 Power Electronics in Small Wind Turbines

  • 4.1. Small wind turbines: components, working, geared, direct-drive wind turbines
  • 4.2. Need for hybrid systems- range and type of hybrid power generating systems
  • 4.3. Wind-PV maximum power point tracking.

Unit 5 Power Electronics in Biomass and Micro Hydro Power Generation

  • 5.1. Gas producing process from Waste: Incineration, Gasification, Thermal De- Polymerization, Pyrolysis, Induction Heating, Plasma arc gasification
  • 5.2. Solid Waste based power generation plants and their capacities currently in India and world.
  • 5.3. Features of Micro Hydro power plants Power Electronics for Renewable Energy Course Code: 3362401 GTU/ NITTTR Bhopal/14 Gujarat State 4
