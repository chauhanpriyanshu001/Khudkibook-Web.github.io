Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics-1 Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics-1 (subject code BE03005011) as prescribed for Chemical · Semester 3 divides the course into 7 units, covering 50 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE03005011)

Unit 1 INTRODUCTION AND FIRST LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS

6 lecture hours

  • The scope of thermodynamics
  • Dimensions and units
  • Measures of amount or size
  • heat, etc
  • Internal Energy
  • The first law of thermodynamics
  • Thermodynamic state
  • state functions
  • Energy balance for closed systems
  • The Phase rule
  • The reversible process
  • Heat capacity
  • Application of first law of thermodynamics to steady state flow process

Unit 2 VOLUMETRIC PROPERTIES OF PURE FLUIDS

8 lecture hours

  • PVT behavior of pure substances
  • Ideal and non-ideal gases
  • Equation of states
  • Vander Waals EOS, Redlich/Kwong (RK) EOS etc.
  • Calculation of constants in terms of Pc, Tc, Vc
  • Generalized Correlations for gases and liquids

Unit 3 HEAT EFFECTS

6 lecture hours

  • Sensible heat effects
  • Temperature dependence of the heat capacity
  • Latent heats of pure substances
  • Approximate methods for the estimation of the latent heat of vaporization
  • Standard heat of reaction
  • Standard heat of formation
  • Standard heat of combustion
  • Temperature Dependence of ΔH0
  • Heat effects of Industrial Reactions

Unit 4 SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS

8 lecture hours

  • Statements of second law of thermodynamics
  • Heat engines
  • Thermodynamic Temperature Scales
  • Concept of entropy
  • Entropy changes of an Ideal Gas, Third law of thermodynamics

Unit 5 THERMODYNAMIC PROPERTIES OF FLUIDS

7 lecture hours

  • The fundamental property relations for homogeneous phases
  • Maxwell’s equations
  • Residual properties
  • Mathematical relations among thermodynamic properties
  • Two phase systems
  • Thermodynamic diagrams

Unit 6 THERMODYNAMICS OF FLOW PROCESS

4 lecture hours

  • Fundamental equations and relationships
  • flow in pipes
  • maximum velocity in pipe flow
  • Single and Multistage compressors and ejectors

Unit 7 REFRIGERATION AND LIQUEFACTION

6 lecture hours

  • Carnot refrigerator
  • Vapor compression cycle
  • Absorption refrigeration
  • Choice of refrigerant
  • Heat pump
  • Liquefaction processes
