Modern Physics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Modern Physics (subject code DI01000061) as prescribed for Nano · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 55 listed topics.   • percentage error
  • rounding off, significant digits
