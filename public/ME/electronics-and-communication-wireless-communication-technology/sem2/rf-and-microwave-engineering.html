RF and Microwave Engineering Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for RF and Microwave Engineering (subject code 2724404) as prescribed for EAC( · Semester 2 divides the course into 8 units, covering 44 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2724404)

Unit 1 Transmission Line Theory

3 lecture hours

  • Lumpled Element circuit model
  • Field Analysis of Transmission line
  • Terminated Lossless line
  • Smith Chart
  • The Quarter-Wave Transformer
  • Generator and Load Mismatches
  • Lossy Transmission line

Unit 2 Transmission Line and Waveguides

7 lecture hours

  • General Solutions of TEM, TE, and TM waves
  • Parallel Plate Waveguides
  • Rectangular Waveguides
  • Circular Waveguides
  • Wave Velocities and Dispersion
  • summary of Transmission line and waveguides

Unit 3 Microwave Network Analysis

4 lecture hours

  • Impedance and equivalent voltage and currents
  • Impedance and Admittance Matrices
  • Scattering Matrix
  • ABCD Matrix
  • Signal Flow Graphs

Unit 4 Impedance Matching and tuning

4 lecture hours

  • Matching with lumped elements
  • Single and Double Stub Matching
  • Quarter wave transformer
  • Theory of small reflections

Unit 5 Microwave Resonators

4 lecture hours

  • Series and Parallel Resonant Circuits
  • Transmission Line Resonators
  • Rectangular and Circular waveguide cavities

Unit 6 Power Divider and Directional Coupler

5 lecture hours

  • Basic Properties of divider and couplers
  • T-junction divider
  • Wilkinson power divider
  • waveguide directional coupler
  • quadrature hybrid
  • Coupled line directional coupler
  • The Lange coupler
  • Ferrite Isolator
  • Ferrite Phase shifter
  • Ferrite circulators

Unit 7 Microwave Filters

5 lecture hours

  • Periodic Structures
  • Filter design by image parameter method
  • Filter design by insertion loss method
  • Filter transformations
  • Filter implementations

Unit 8 Introduction to Microwave System

4 lecture hours

  • System aspect of antenna
  • Microwave communication system
  • Radar system
  • Microwave Propagation
