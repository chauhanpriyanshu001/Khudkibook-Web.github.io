Tractor and Farm Equipment Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Tractor and Farm Equipment (subject code DI05002061) as prescribed for COMPUTER · Semester 5 divides the course into 5 units, covering 71 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Introduction to tractors

5 lecture hours

  • 1.1 History of Tractors.  Origin of tractors.  Founder of tractors.  Pre-mechanization and early mechanization: animal-drawn implements
  • need for mechanization. 1.2 Definition and functions of tractor. 1.3 Classification of tractors.  Based on power
  • application  Wheel
  • utility tractors  Electric
  • hybrid and autonomous tractors 1.4 Comparison of tractors with road automobiles

Unit 2 Tractor construction and major systems

15 lecture hours

  • 2.1 General layout of a farm tractor. 2.2 Types of tractor engines: Gasoline engine
  • diesel engine
  • CNG engines
  • biofuel engines
  • electric engines, and hybrid engines. 2.3 Tractor clutches: Single-plate
  • split (independent PTO) 2.4 Tractor Transmission: Basic: Sliding mesh
  • constant mesh, and synchromesh gearbox
  • Advance and automatic: Collar-shift
  • power shuttle
  • powershift/semi powershift
  • hydrostatic (HST), CVT, range selector
  • creeper gears. 2.5 Differential and Final Drive: Bull gear
  • planetary final drive
  • differential lock. 2.6 Steering system: Manual and power steering in tractors. 2.7 Braking system: Mechanical
  • oil-immersed brakes
  • independent wheel braking for turning. 2.8 Tractor controls and instruments: hand/foot throttle
  • gear levers
  • PTO lever
  • hydraulic levers
  • differential lock pedal
  • gauges and warning lights

Unit 3 Tractor hydraulic system, hitching, PTO and Farm implements

  • 3.1 Tractor Hydraulics: Types

  • mixed control
  • Automatic Depth and Draft Control (ADDC) and Electric Quick Raise and Lower (EQRL) and Directional Control Valve (DCV). 3.3 Tractor Hitches: Three-point hitch
  • drawbar hitch
  • quick hitch systems, and front hitches. 3.4 Power Take-Off (PTO): Types (independent PTO, live PTO, transmission PTO, split shaft PTO)
  • PTO shaft splines and speeds (540/1000 rpm). 3.5 Tractor ballast and traction: front and rear ballast
  • tyre selection
  • inflation pressure
  • wheel weights and liquid ballast
  • effect on tractive efficiency and soil compaction. 3.6 Farm implements:  Primary tillage: mould board plough
  • disc plough
  • chisel plough
  • subsoiler.  Secondary tillage: cultivators
  • harrows (disc
  • puddler.  Sowing/planting: seed drill
  • seed-cum-fertilizer drill
  • planter.  Intercultural and plant protection equipment: tractor-drawn cultivators
  • rotary weeders
  • sprayers and dusters.  Hitching of implements to tractor: mounted
  • trailed implements and tipping adjustment: safe hitching practices

Unit 4 Tractor Operation, maintenance, and safety

5 lecture hours

  • 4.1 Tractor operation: Pre-start checks
  • starting and stopping procedures
  • basic field operation techniques for different implements. 4.2 Tractor Maintenance: Daily/periodic maintenance schedule
  • lubrication charts
  • filter service
  • cooling system checks
  • tyre care, and storage. 4.3 Implement maintenance: Replacement of wear parts (shares
  • adjustments and off-season storage. 4.4 Safety: General safety rules in field and road operation
  • roll-over hazards and stability
  • basic legal provisions related to dangerous machines and road traffic for tractors and trailers
