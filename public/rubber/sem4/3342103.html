Testing Of Metals Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Testing Of Metals (subject code 3342103) as prescribed for Rubber · Semester 4 divides the course into 4 units, covering 49 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3342103)

Unit 1 Importance of Testing

  • 1.1. Importance of testing of materials.
  • 1.2. Types of testing.
  • 1.3. Merits and demerits of destructive test (DT) and non-destructive test (NDT).
  • 1.4. Importance of IS and ASTM standards.
  • 1.5. IS and ASTM standards for various DT and NDT .

Unit 2 Destructive Testing

  • 2.1. List the destructive test.
  • 2.2. Explain stress-strain diagram.
  • 2.3. Tensile testing: Importance, procedure,calculation, operation and co- relation with other test.
  • 2.4. Compression testing: Importance, procedure.
  • 2.5. Hardness testing: Importance, types of testing, procedure, applications.
  • 2.6. Impact testing: Importance, procedure, types of test.
  • 2.7. Fatigue testing: Explain endurance limit, procedure.
  • 2.8. Creep testing: Importance, procedure.
  • 2.9. Cupping test: Importance, procedure.

Unit 3 Non- Destructive Testing

  • 3.1. Types of non-destructive test.
  • 3.2. Visual inspection: Application, Merits and demerits.
  • 3.3. Procedure, merits, demerits and application of Liquid penetrant test, Magnetic particle test, Ultrasonic test, X-ray and Gamma ray radiography, Eddy current test.

Unit 4 Characterizatio n of metals and alloys

  • 4.1. Importance of materials characterisation
  • 4.2. Classification of material characterisation techniques.
  • 4.3. Introduction, principle and application of TGA, DSC, SEM, TEM, XRF, XRD, AAS and AES Testing of Metals Course code: 3342103 GTU/NITTTR/Bhopal/13-14 Gujarat State 3
