The official GTU curriculum for Hydraulics (subject code 4330604) as prescribed for CIVIL · Semester 3 divides the course into 4 units, covering 17 listed topics. Unit 1 Pressure measureme nt and Hydrostatic pressure

  • 1.7.1. Computation of Total Pressure and depth of the center of pressure

Unit 2 Hydro kinematics & Hydrodyna mics

  • 2.1. Discharge & its units, Continuity Equation
  • 2.2. Types of flow - Laminar --Turbulent --Uniform -- Non-uniform –Steady- -Un-steady –Rotational and irrotational --One, Two and Three Dimensional flow
  • 2.3. Reynold’s number
  • 2.4. Types of Energy – Potential, Pressure, and kinematics
  • 2.5. Bernoulli’s theorem: statement, assumptions, derivation & limitations.

  • 3.2. Major Head losses in pipe: Computation by Darcy’s Weisbach equation, Use of Chezy’s & Manning’s formula, Nomograms 3.3Computation of minor head losses in a pipe.
  • 3.4. Hydraulic Gradient Line (HGL) and Total Energy Line ( TEL)
  • 3.5. Flow through pipes in series (Compound Pipe), pipes in parallel.
  • 3.6. Equivalent Pipe 3.7Discharge measurement using orifice
  • 3.8. Various Hydraulic Coefficient and its relation
