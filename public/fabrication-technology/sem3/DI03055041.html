Fabrication Technology-2 Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Fabrication Technology-2 (subject code DI03055041) as prescribed for FT · Semester 3 divides the course into 5 units, covering 21 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (DI03055041)

Unit 1 Introduction to process equipment

7 lecture hours

  • scope & importance of process equipment in industries. 1.2 Definition
  • classification & function of different process equipments. 1.3 Manufacturing terminology of pressure vessel. 1.4 Sequence of process equipments fabrication. 1.5 Fundamental units and its conversion. 1.6 Volume capacity calculation of pressure vessel. 1.7 Introduction & scope of different codes/standards used in process equipment fabrication industries. 1.8 Qualities of shop floor supervisor in process equipment fabrication industries. 1.9 Major process equipment fabrication industries in India

Unit 2 Process equipment drawing

9 lecture hours

  • 2.1 Introduction of process equipment drawing. 2.2 Types of process equipment drawing. 2.3 Study of title block in process equipment drawing. 2.4 General notes & bill of material in GA drawing. 2.5 Design data table. 2.6 Typical pressure vessel drawing with its terminology. 2.7 Detailing of process equipment drawing. 2.8 Weld joint geometry/nomenclature/ terminology/types & weld symbols. 2.9 Detail drawing. 2.10 Nozzle schedule table. 2.11 Shell & Cone layout developments and related examples

Unit 3 Shell, Cone & Dish end fabrication

12 lecture hours

  • 3.1 Introduction of shell fabrication. 3.2 Plate length calculation & marking for shell fabrication. 3.3 Advanced cutting processes (plasma cutting
  • water jet cutting
  • laser cutting) 3.4 Shell forming from plate (Plate rolling) 3.5 Longitudinal seam fit up & set up of shell 3.6 Run in, run out plate & PTC for shell. 3.7 Inspection of plate & shell. 3.8 Definition
  • introduction & types of cones or reducer
  • Program Name: Engineering Level: Diploma Branch: Fabrication Technology Course / Subject Code: DI03055041 Course / Subject Name: Fabrication Technology-2 w.e.f. 2024-25 http://syllabus.gtu.ac.in/ Page no.3 of 8 3.9 Cone fabrication. 3.10 Inspection of cone. 3.11 Definition & Introduction of heads or dish ends. 3.12 Types of dish end. 3.13 Factors affecting selection of dish end. 3.14 Manufacturing processes of dish ends. 3.15 Blank diameter calculation of dish ends. 3.16 Development of hemispherical dish ends. 3.17 Different marking in dish ends. 3.18 Inspection of dish end. 3.19 Shell
  • cone & dish end forming defects. 3.20 Safe operating procedures for plasma cutting & forming(rolling) operation

Unit 4 Process equipment attachments

9 lecture hours

  • 4.1 Definition & introduction & function of nozzles. 4.2 Types of nozzles 4.3 Nozzle elevation
  • height & centerline marking. 4.4 Nozzle orientation calculations. 4.5 Definition
  • function & types of flanges. 4.6 Flange marking & calculations. 4.7 Process equipments supports. 4.8 Reinforcement pad & telltale hole. 4.9 Introduction & function of limpet coil 4.10 Limpet coil marking on shell. 4.11 Lifting lug/lifting trunnion 4.12 External & internals of process equipment like insulation cleat
  • ladder supports
  • platform cleat
  • trays and tray support ring 4.13 Tube bundle fabrication for heat exchanger. 4.14 Elements of tube bundle like Tube Sheet
  • Tie Rod, Spacers
  • Impingement Plate

Unit 5 Assembly of process equipment

8 lecture hours

  • 5.1 Shell to shell fit up & set up. 5.2 Ovality calculation of shell. 5.3 Dish end to shell fit up & set up 5.4 Nozzle to shell fit up & set up 5.5 Nozzle to dish end fit up & set up 5.6 Shell to support fit and set up 5.7 Equipment & tools for process equipment fabrication like tank rotator
  • spider etc.
  • 5.8 Jigs & fixtures used for process equipment
