Engineering Economics and Management Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Engineering Economics and Management (subject code 2130004) as prescribed for Biomed · Semester 3 divides the course into 10 units, covering 55 listed topics. 4 lecture hours

  • law of supply
  • equilibrium between demand & supply Elasticity

  • price elasticity
  • income elasticity
  • cross elasticity

4 lecture hours

  • (meaning & characteristics of Land

  • fixed cost
  • variable cost
  • total cost
  • average cost
  • marginal cost
  • opportunity cost
  • Break even analysis

  • stock and flow concept

4 lecture hours

  • measures to reduce Unemployment: meaning

4 lecture hours

  • tools Banking

  • repo rate
  • reverse repo rate, SLR

4 lecture hours

  • types and roles of managers Management Principles
  • Scientific principles

5 lecture hours

  • nature and importance) Organizational Structures
  • principles of organization

  • line & staff
  • hybrid (explanation with merits and demerits)
  • span of control

Unit 8 Introduction to Marketing management; Marketing Mix, concepts of marketing,

5 lecture hours

  • market segmentation Introduction to Finance Management
