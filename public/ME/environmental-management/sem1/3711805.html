Groundwater Hydrology & Contamination Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Groundwater Hydrology & Contamination (subject code 3711805) as prescribed for EM · Semester 1 divides the course into 8 units, covering 35 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3711805)

Unit 1 Introduction

4 lecture hours

  • Definition of ground water
  • vertical distribution of sub surface water
  • hydrological properties of water bearing strata
  • ground water in hydrologic cycle

Unit 2 Ground Water Hydraulics

12 lecture hours

  • Darcy’s law, its range of validity
  • Dupuit’s & Forchheimer assumptions
  • Applications of Darcy’s law for simple flow systems
  • Governing differential equations for confined and unconfined aquifers
  • steady and unsteady flow solutions for fully penetrating wells
  • partially penetrating wells
  • interference of wells
  • test pumping analysis with steady and unsteady flows
  • delayed yield
  • method of images

Unit 3 Ground Water Quality

2 lecture hours

  • Indian and international standards

Unit 4 Ground Water Pollution

3 lecture hours

  • remedial and preventive measures

Unit 5 Transport of Ground Water Contamination

3 lecture hours

  • Transport Mechanisms
  • Dispersion and diffusion
  • Numerical Flow and Transport Modeling

Unit 6 Models for Groundwater Flow

12 lecture hours

  • Sampling and Monitoring Methods
  • Transport Mechanisms
  • Modeling Advective-Dispersive Transport
  • Adsorption and Chemical Reaction
  • Biodegradation Kinetics
  • Numerical Flow and Transport Modeling
  • Waste Site Characterization/Investigation
  • Ground Water Remediation
  • Legal Issues in Groundwater Contamination

Unit 7 Ground Water Restoration and Treatment

3 lecture hours

  • Source control strategies
  • Treatment technologies
  • In situ treatment methods
  • Pump and treat method

Unit 8 Ground Water Conservation

  • Ground Water Budget
  • Seepage From Surface Water
  • Artificial Recharge 3 7
