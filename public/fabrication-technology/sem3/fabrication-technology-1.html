Fabrication Technology - 1 Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Fabrication Technology - 1 (subject code 4335504) as prescribed for FT · Semester 3 divides the course into 5 units, covering 46 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (4335504)

Unit 1 Introduction to Structural Fabrication

  • 1.1. Need, scope & importance, layout of structural fabrication industry.
  • 1.2. Definition & classification of structural fabrication.
  • 1.3. List & function of structural fabrication processes/operations
  • 1.4. Application of structural fabrication.
  • 1.5. Scope, need & importance of codes and standards.
  • 1.6. Importance and introduction of structural fabrication codes & standards like BIS – 800, AWS D1.1, BIS: 226, BIS- 2062.
  • 1.7. Role of Third-Party Inspection (TPI) agencies in structural fabrication industry.
  • 1.8. Need attitude & skill require for shop- floor supervisor.

Unit 2 Structural fabrication drawing and materials

  • 2.1. Introduction of structural fabrication drawing.
  • 2.2. Types of structural fabrication drawing.
  • 2.3. General notes & bill of material in GA drawing.
  • 2.4. Introduction of pre-engineered building (PEB)
  • 2.5. Detailing of structures, terminology/ various parts used in structural fabrication drawing.
  • 2.6. Various types of joints & fit up-set up.
  • 2.7. Typical members of industrial buildings & roof truss.
  • 2.8. Welding symbols represented in structural drawing.
  • 2.9. Symbols for fasteners used for structural fabrication.
  • 2.10. Structural fabrication general arrangement drawing.
  • 2.11. Structural fabrication detailed drawing.
  • 2.12. Introduction, classification, advantages of structural steel.
  • 2.13. Commercially available forms/profile sections of metals.
  • 2.14. Material Test Certificate (MTC)

Unit 3 Structural fabrication

  • 3.1. Definition and unit conversion of Area, Volume, Density, Mass, Weight.
  • 3.2. Problems related to Area, Volume, FABRICATION TECHNOLOGY-1 Course Code: 4335504 calculation & marking different commercial forms used in structural fabrication.
  • 3.3. Use of different mensuration formulas.
  • 3.4. Cost estimation of structural job.
  • 3.5. Trigonometry function and related exercise/calculation for structural fabrication
  • 3.6. Geometrical construction used for marking of large size jobs

Unit 4 Structural cutting and Joining Processes

  • 4.1. Introduction and Classification of cutting processes used in structural fabrication.
  • 4.2. Working principle, setup sketch, advantages, disadvantages and application for:
  • 4.2.1. Sawing (Manual/hacksaw machine /bandsaw machine)
  • 4.2.3. Oxy fuel cutting
  • 4.2.4. Air arc gouging
  • 4.3. Introduction of joining process
  • 4.4. Classification of joining process
  • 4.5. Selection of joining method.
  • 4.6. Introduction, types, terminology, process, advantages and dis advantages of riveted joints.
  • 4.7. Introduction, types, terminology, process, advantages and dis advantages of bolted joints.
  • 4.8. Safety in cutting shop.

Unit 5 Tools, Equipment, machinery & accessories for fabrication

  • 5.2. Classification of tools, equipment, accessories & machineries used for structural fabrication.
  • 5.3. Work holding tools.
  • 5.4. Marking and measuring tools
  • 5.5. Cutting tools.
  • 5.6. Finishing/fitting tools.
  • 5.7. Power, portable and other tools.
