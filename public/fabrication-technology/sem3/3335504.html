WELDING TECHNOLOGY-I Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for WELDING TECHNOLOGY-I (subject code 3335504) as prescribed for FT · Semester 3 divides the course into 9 units, covering 62 listed topics.   • 1.1. Need, Scope & importance of welding in industries.
