Energy Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Energy Technology (subject code 2170505) as prescribed for Chemical · Semester 7 divides the course into 13 units, covering 107 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2170505)

Unit 1 An Introduction to Energy Sources

2 lecture hours

  • Energy sources (conventional & non-conventional)
  • renewable energy resources
  • primary & secondary energy sources
  • energy chain
  • energy demand
  • national energy strategy & plan
  • energy management
  • energy audit & conservation

Unit 2 Definitions, Units & Measures

3 lecture hours

  • Proximate & ultimate analysis
  • calorific values
  • rank of coal
  • coking & caking
  • basis for reporting results of analysis
  • units & conversion factors

Unit 3 Solid Fuels

3 lecture hours

  • Wood & charcoal
  • sub-bituminous & bituminous coals
  • semianthracite and anthracite coals
  • cannel & boghead coal
  • origin of coal
  • composition of coal
  • analysis & properties of coal

Unit 4 Processing of Solid Fuels

4 lecture hours

  • Coal preparation
  • washability curve
  • dry & wet washing methods of coal
  • washer efficiency
  • gasification & liquefaction of solid fuels

Unit 5 Solar Energy

4 lecture hours

  • Solar radiation & related terms
  • measurement of solar radiation
  • solar energy collectors – flat plate collector
  • air collector
  • collectors with porous absorbers
  • concentrating collectors
  • applications & advantages of various collectors
  • selective absorber coatings
  • solar energy storage systems (thermal
  • chemical & mechanical)
  • solar pond
  • applications of solar energy

Unit 6 Wind Energy

4 lecture hours

  • Basic principles
  • power in wind
  • force on blades & turbines
  • wind energy conversion
  • site selection
  • basic components of wind energy conversion systems (WECS)
  • classification of WECS
  • wind energy collectors
  • applications of wind energy

Unit 7 Energy from Biomass

5 lecture hours

  • energy plantation
  • biomass conversion technologies
  • biogas generation
  • factors affecting biogas generation
  • classification of biogas plants & their comparisons
  • types of biogas plants
  • biogas from plant wastes
  • community plants & site selection
  • digester design considerations
  • design calculations
  • methods of maintaining & starting biogas plants
  • properties & utilisation of biogas
  • thermal gasification of biomass
  • alternative liquid fuels

Unit 8 Geothermal Energy

5 lecture hours

  • Geothermal resources
  • hydrothermal resources
  • liquid dominated systems
  • geopressured resources
  • petrothermal systems
  • magma resources
  • energy conservation & comparison with other resources
  • applications of geothermal energy

Unit 9 Energy from Oceans

4 lecture hours

  • methods (open cycle & close cycle) energy from tides
  • components of tidal power plants
  • methods of utilization of tidal energy
  • ocean waves
  • wave energy conversion devices

Unit 10 Fuel Cell

5 lecture hours

  • hydrogen – oxygen fuel cell
  • ion exchange membrane cell
  • fossil fuel cell
  • molten carbonate cell
  • advantages & disadvantages
  • conversion efficiency
  • type of electrodes
  • applications of fuel cells

Unit 11 Hydrogen & Methanol

  • Properties of Hydrogen
  • production and application of 05 9 hydrogen
  • thermochemical methods
  • fossil fuel methods
  • solar methods
  • storage & transportation
  • safety & management

Unit 12 Magneto Hydro-Dynamic (MHD) Power Generation

5 lecture hours

  • MHD system
  • open cycle system
  • closed cycle system
  • design problems & developments
  • materials for MHD generators
  • magnetic field & super conductivity

Unit 13 Nuclear Energy

5 lecture hours

  • fuel for nuclear fission reactor (exploration
  • fuel fabrication
  • fuel use, reprocessing
  • waste disposal)
  • Nuclear fuel cycle
  • storage & transportation
  • uranium enrichment process
  • nuclear reactor power plant
  • fast breeder reactor
  • boiling water reactor
  • pressurised heavy & light water reactor
  • Nuclear waste management
