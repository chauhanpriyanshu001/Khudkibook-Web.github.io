GTU Summer 2026 Results Declared: How To Check Your Scorecard & Apply For Re-Check

20 September 2026 5 min read GTU News, Result

Gujarat Technological University (GTU) has declared the Summer 2026 examination results in a phased manner. Results for the Diploma Semester 2 Regular and BE Semester 3 Remedial (May 2026) examinations were released on 24 August 2026, and further batches — including several BE and BB semesters — were announced in early September. This post shows you exactly how to check your scorecard, and how to apply for re-check or re-assessment before the deadline.

Which Results Have Been Declared?

GTU has been releasing Summer 2026 results programme-by-programme. Recent batches include:

  • Diploma Semester 2 Regular (May 2026) — released 24 August 2026.
  • BE Semester 3 Remedial (May 2026) — released 24 August 2026.
  • M.Pharm Sem 2 Regular & Remedial, BE Sem 4 Regular, Diploma in Vocation — announced mid-to-late August 2026.
  • Multiple BE and BB semester results — announced on 8 September 2026.

If your programme's result is not listed yet, do not worry. GTU declares results in phases, and you should keep checking the official portals below.

How To Check Your GTU Summer 2026 Result (3 Easy Ways)

Your scorecard is available on any of these official portals — no third-party app is needed.

  1. GTU result portal — open gturesults.in, select your examination and session, enter your enrolment or seat number with the captcha, then submit.
  2. GTU official website — go to gtu.ac.in → Results, pick the relevant course result link, and log in with your enrolment number, password and captcha.
  3. GTU Student Portal — log in at student.gtu.ac.in, open the Examination section and download your provisional marksheet.
Download and keep a PDF copy of your marksheet immediately. Re-check applications and many college processes ask for your result documents.

Re-Check & Re-Assessment: How It Works

Not happy with a subject grade? GTU lets you apply for a re-check (the paper is totalled and checked again for missed corrections) or a re-assessment (a fresh examiner re-evaluates the answer sheet).

Application Deadlines

  • For the results released on 24 August, the re-check / re-assessment window closed on 27 August 2026.
  • For the batch announced on 8 September, applications must be submitted by 11 September 2026.
Every declaration has its own window — check the exact date on the GTU circular for your result before applying. Missing it means waiting for the next cycle.

Re-Check / Re-Assessment Fees

ServiceFee
Re-checking (per subject)₹100
Re-assessment (per subject)₹250

Pay through SBI Collect: select Educational Institutions → search for Gujarat Technological University → choose the Recheck / Reassessment Fee option. UPI, net banking, debit and credit cards are accepted. Save the payment receipt.

Steps To Apply

  1. Log in to the GTU Student Portal with your enrolment number and password.
  2. Open the Exam section and select Re-check / Re-assessment Application.
  3. Choose the subjects, submit, and note down the application number.
  4. Pay the fee on SBI Collect using the application number.

GTU Grading Scale & SPI / CPI

GTU follows a 10-point grading system. SPI (Semester Performance Index) measures one semester; CPI (Cumulative Performance Index) measures your overall performance across all semesters completed.
