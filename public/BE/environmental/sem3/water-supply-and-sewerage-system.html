Water Supply and Sewerage System Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Water Supply and Sewerage System (subject code BE03013011) as prescribed for Env. Engg · Semester 3 divides the course into 15 units, covering 65 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE03013011)

Unit 1 Water supply scheme

2 lecture hours

  • Importance & necessity of water supply scheme
  • Importance and reliability of water works
  • essentials of water supply engineering

Unit 2 Sources of water

2 lecture hours

  • Surface water sources
  • ground water sources and terminology related to ground water
  • comparison of different sources based on quality and quantity

Unit 3 Demand of water quantity

2 lecture hours

  • Types of demand
  • Per capita demand
  • design period
  • fluctuation in demand of water
  • factors affecting demand of water

Unit 4 Pumps and Pumping stations

4 lecture hours

  • Need of pumping
  • classification of pumps
  • different type of pumps used in water supply
  • power of pumping
  • total lift of pump
  • H.P of pump
  • location of pumping station
  • site selection

Unit 5 Collection and conveyance

2 lecture hours

  • type of intake
  • conveyance of water
  • different type of pipes used in water supply
  • laying of pipe
  • hydrostatic test

Unit 6 Distribution system

4 lecture hours

  • Type of distribution system
  • different layout of distribution system
  • methods of supplying water
  • pressures in distribution system
  • distribution resources and its capacity
  • type of reservoirs & accessories

Unit 7 Valves and Fittings

4 lecture hours

  • Different type of valves
  • stop cock & water tap, pipe fittings
  • leakage & waste of water factors
  • affecting losses & wastes

Unit 8 Sanitary works

2 lecture hours

  • sanitary works
  • objectives of sewage disposal

Unit 9 Systems of sanitation

2 lecture hours

  • Methods of collection
  • conservancy systems
  • collection system
  • water carriage system
  • sewerage system Program Name: Bachelor of Engineering Level: UG Branch: Environmental Engineering Subject Code: BE03013011 Course / Subject Name: Water Supply and Sewerage System

Unit 10 Quantity of sanitary and storm sewage

4 lecture hours

  • Sources of sanitary sewage
  • factors affecting and determination of quantity of sanitary sewage
  • factor affecting storm sewage and determination of quantity of storm sewage

Unit 11 Design of sewers

4 lecture hours

  • Design period
  • per capita sewage flow
  • ground water infiltration
  • estimation of storm runoff
  • flow assumption
  • determination of velocity of flow

Unit 12 Drains and sewers

2 lecture hours

  • sewers: sections
  • sewer material
  • sewer drawings
  • corrosion prevention in sewers

Unit 13 Sewers appurtenances

5 lecture hours

  • street inlet
  • flushing tanks
  • catch basins
  • inverted siphon
  • ventilation of sewers

Unit 14 Construction and maintenance of sewers

3 lecture hours

  • Lying of sewers
  • jointing of sewers, and hydraulic testing of pipe sewers
  • maintenance of sewers
  • sewer cleaning equipments and devices

Unit 15 House plumbing

3 lecture hours

  • Plumbing tools
  • traps and system of plumbing
