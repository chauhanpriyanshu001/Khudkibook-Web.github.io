Process Modeling and Identification Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Process Modeling and Identification (subject code ME01067041) as prescribed for IACI · Semester 1 divides the course into 7 units, covering 28 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01067041)

Unit 1 Process Modeling

4 lecture hours

  • State-space model: General form and canonical form
  • Input/output model: transfer function and coprime polynomials
  • Plant Parametric model: Linear and bilinear parametric models

Unit 2 Introduction to process identification

4 lecture hours

  • Frequency domain expression
  • Program Name: Engineering Level: PG Branch: Applied Instrumentation Course / Subject Code : ME01067041 Course / Subject Name : Process Modeling and Identification w.e.f. 2024-25 http://syllabus.gtu.ac.in/ Page no. 1 of 2 signal spectra
  • model structure
  • least square method
  • procedure steps for process identification

Unit 3 Identification of LTI process

8 lecture hours

  • sets of linear models
  • transfer function models
  • state-space models
  • distributed parameter models
  • model structures and identifiability

Unit 4 Identification of Time varying and nonlinear systems

8 lecture hours

  • linear time- varying models
  • models with nonlinearities
  • nonlinear state space models
  • Black box models

Unit 5 Nonparametric time and frequency domain methods

8 lecture hours

  • transient response and correlation analysis
  • frequency response analysis
  • Fourier analysis
  • spectral analysis

Unit 6 Parametric estimation methods

  • Minimizing prediction errors
  • Linear regressions
  • least squares method
  • maximum Likelihood method 6 10%

Unit 7 Convergence and consistency

  • Conditions on the data set, Prediction- error approach
  • consistency and identifiability
  • correlation approach 4 10%
