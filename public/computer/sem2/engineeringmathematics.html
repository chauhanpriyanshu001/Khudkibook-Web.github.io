Engineering Mathematics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Engineering Mathematics (subject code 4320002) as prescribed for COMPUTER · Semester 2 divides the course into 3 units, covering 21 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (4320002)

Unit 1 Matrices

  • 1.1. Concept of Matrix
  • 1.2. Types of Matrices
  • 1.3. Addition, Subtraction and multiplication by scalar of matrices
  • 1.4. Product of two matrices
  • 1.5. Adjoint and Inverse of a matrix of order 2X2 and 3X3.
  • 1.6. Solution of Simultaneous linear equations of two variables.

Unit 2 Differentiati on and its Applications

  • 2.1. Concept and Definition of Differentiation
  • 2.2. Working rules: Sum, Product, Division
  • 2.3. Chain Rule
  • 2.4. Derivative of Implicit functions
  • 2.5. Derivative of Parametric functions
  • 2.6. Logarithmic Differentiation
  • 2.7. Successive Differentiation up to second order
  • 2.8. Applications: Velocity, Acceleration, Maxima & Minima of given simple functions.

Unit 3 Integration and its Applications

  • 3.1. Concept and Definition of Integration.
  • 3.2. Working rules and Integral of standard functions.
  • 3.3. Method of substitution.
  • 3.4. Integration by parts.
  • 3.5. Definite Integral and its properties.
  • 3.6. Applications: Area and volume. (Simple problems) Unit– IV 4a. Find the order and degree of differential equations.
  • 4.1. Concept and Definition, Order and Degree of differential equation. Engineering Mathematics Course Code: 4320002
