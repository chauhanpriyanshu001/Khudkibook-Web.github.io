About GTU Production Engineering (ME)

This page collects every semester of GTU Production Engineering ME that we have on file, with the subject code, credit value and total marks for each one. It is meant to be the single tab you keep open while the semester runs, so you can check a code against your timetable, open the syllabus for a unit, and pull the last few years of papers without searching for them again.

Production Engineering at GTU is a semester-wise programme. The early semesters carry the shared engineering foundation — mathematics, applied physics or chemistry, and communication skills — while the later semesters move into the branch's own core and electives. The subject table on each semester page reflects that split, so you can see at a glance which papers are core and which are electives.

Students of Production Engineering usually head into one of a few directions after graduation: a technical role in an engineering firm, a software or systems role if the degree carried enough computing, or further study through GATE, an ME programme, or a competitive exam. Which direction suits you is easier to judge once you know which subjects you actually enjoyed, and the semester pages are the fastest way to find that out.

What Is In Production Engineering At GTU

  • Previous year question papers

    Papers are linked straight from the official GTU question-paper archive wherever one exists. Solving the last four or five sittings of a paper is the single most reliable way to learn GTU's question pattern and the marks weightage it actually uses.

  • Textbook links where they exist

    Some subjects have a full textbook PDF attached. Where one is not available yet, the button says so plainly instead of pretending otherwise. The official syllabus PDF is always one click away so you can buy or borrow the right edition.

  • Unit-wise syllabus breakdown

    The unit list on each subject page comes from the GTU syllabus document itself. Use it to turn a whole subject into a checklist of units and tick them off as you revise.

  • One-tap sharing

    Every page has a share button. Send the subject page to your group instead of forwarding a mystery PDF link, so everyone lands on the same, current version.

How To Prepare Well In Production Engineering

  • Tip 1

    Start every semester by reading the official syllabus unit list once, end to end, before opening any textbook. It takes ten minutes and tells you exactly how much ground the paper covers.

  • Tip 2

    Attempt at least one full previous year paper under a two-hour clock before your first revision round. The gap between what you can write and what the paper asks is your real revision list.

  • Tip 3

    Keep the credit value and total marks in view while planning. A four-credit subject deserves roughly twice the revision time of a two-credit one, and students routinely get that ratio backwards.

  • Tip 4

    Re-attempt every paper you got wrong, a week later, from a blank sheet. Re-reading a marked paper feels like revision but is closer to recognition.

How To Use This Page

  1. 1

    Pick your branch and semester

    Start from the home page, choose Diploma, BE or ME, then your branch. Each branch page lists every semester with the number of subjects it holds, so you can jump straight to the semester you are sitting.

  2. 2

    Read the subject table

    The semester page opens with a table of subject codes, names, credits and total marks. Match the codes against your GTU exam timetable or hall ticket so you download the right paper.

  3. 3

    Open a subject and take the papers

    On the subject page, use the Paper button for previous year papers and the Syllabus button for the official GTU syllabus PDF. The Book button is enabled only when a textbook PDF is actually attached.

  4. 4

    Attempt papers before you revise

    Attempt a past paper closed-book first, then mark it against the syllabus units you have not covered yet. That tells you what to revise far faster than reading a textbook cover to cover.

Questions GTU Students Ask

Is Khudkibook free to use?

Yes. Every syllabus, textbook link and previous year paper on this site is free, with no login wall and no payment step. The site is run by students, and its only aim is to keep GTU study material in one place that is easy to search.

Are the question papers official GTU papers?

The papers linked from subject pages come from Gujarat Technological University's own question-paper archive. We link to the source rather than hosting a copy, so what you open is the paper as the university published it.

Why does some material say Coming Soon?

We only publish a link once we have verified that the file exists and opens. When a subject has neither a textbook nor a paper on file yet, the page says Coming Soon instead of sending you to a dead link. Those subjects still carry the official syllabus PDF, which is enough to start.

Do you provide solutions or notes for the papers?

We publish the question papers themselves and the official syllabus. Treat the papers as your practice material: attempt them under a timer, then compare answers with your textbook. Group discussion with the same paper is usually more useful than a read-made solution PDF.

Which branches and semesters are covered?

The site covers GTU Diploma, BE Degree and ME programmes across every branch GTU offers, with a semester page for each one. Coverage depth varies by branch because paper availability varies; each semester page shows the exact subject count it currently holds.
