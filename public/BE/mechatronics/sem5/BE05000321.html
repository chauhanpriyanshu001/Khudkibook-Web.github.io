Basics of Industrial Engineering Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Basics of Industrial Engineering (subject code BE05000321) as prescribed for Mechatronics · Semester 5 divides the course into 5 units, covering 76 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE05000321)

Unit 1 Location Selection and Plant Layout

7 lecture hours

  • Nature of Location Decision
  • Importance of Plant Location
  • Dynamic Nature of Plant Location
  • Choice of site for selection
  • Comparison of location
  • Principles of Plant layout and Types
  • factors affecting layout
  • factors governing flow pattern
  • travel chart
  • analytical tools of plant layout
  • layout of manufacturing shop floor
  • repair shop
  • services sectors and process plant
  • Quantitative methods of Plant layout: CRAFT and CORELAP
  • Relationship diagrams

Unit 2 Productivity and Work Study

10 lecture hours

  • Definition of productivity
  • application and advantages of productivity improvement tools
  • reasons for increase and decreases in productivity
  • Areas of application of work study in industry
  • Reaction of management and labour to work study
  • Method Study: Objectives and procedure for methods analysis
  • Recording techniques
  • Operations Process Chart
  • Flow Process Chart
  • Multiple Activity Chart
  • Travel Chart, and Two-Handed process chart
  • String Diagram
  • Micro motion and macro-motion study: Principles of motion economy
  • Normal work areas and work place design
  • Work Measurement: Objectives
  • Work measurement techniques – time study
  • work sampling
  • pre-determined motion time standards (PMTS) Determination of time standards
  • Observed time
  • basic time
  • normal time
  • rating factors
  • allowances, and standard time
  • Introduction to ergonomics

Unit 3 Industrial Legislation

10 lecture hours

  • Need for Industrial legislation
  • Factories act 1948
  • Industrial dispute act 1947
  • The Indian trade unions act 1926
  • Industrial employment act 1946
  • Payment of wage act 1936
  • Workmen compensation act 1923
  • Payment of bonus act 1965
  • Employees provident fund scheme 1952

Unit 4 Inspection and Quality Control

  • Inspection – functions
  • objectives and benefits
  • quality control principles
  • Concepts of quality circles
  • Total quality management
  • Quality assurance
  • Quality audit
  • Basic Concept ISO 9000
  • ISO 14000 and QS 9000
  • Six sigma: Concept
  • Advantage and limitations
  • Statistical Quality Control: SQC Concept
  • variable and attributes
  • normal distribution curves and its property charts for variable and attributes and their applications and interpretation (analysis) process capability
  • Acceptance sampling
  • sampling plans
  • OC curves and AOQ curves. 10 Program Name: Bachelor of Engineering Level: UG Branch: Mechanical
  • Production and Manufacturing Subject Code: BE05000321 Subject Name: Basics of Industrial Engineering

Unit 5 Principles of Lean and Waste Management

8 lecture hours

  • Principles of Lean Manufacturing (LM) – Basic elements of LM– Introduction to LM Tools- Concept of wastes in LM and their narration - stages of 5S and waste elimination – Conventional Manufacturing versus Lean Manufacturing - Need for LM
  • Agile manufacturing - Definition
  • business need
  • conceptual frame work
  • characteristics, and generic features - Approaches to enhance ability in manufacturing - Managing people in agile organization
  • Waste Management: Introduction and meaning of waste
  • Reasons for generation and accumulation of obsolete
  • Surplus and scrap Items
  • Identification and control of waste
  • disposal of scrap
