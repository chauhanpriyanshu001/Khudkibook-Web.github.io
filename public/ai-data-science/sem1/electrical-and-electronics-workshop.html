Electrical and Electronics Workshop Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Electrical and Electronics Workshop (subject code DI01024011) as prescribed for AI & DS · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 52 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (DI01024011)

Unit 1 Electrical & Electronic Components. 1.1 Passive components

8 lecture hours

  • 1.2 Resistors: General-purpose resistors
  • Power resistors
  • Wire-wound resistors
  • Thick film resistors
  • Surface-mount resistors
  • ceramic resistor
  • Axial resistors
  • Precision resistors
  • Variable resistors
  • resistor array
  • standard resistor values. 1.3 Inductors: Iron core
  • Ferrite core
  • Toroidal core inductor
  • Variable with slug
  • Air core. 1.4 Capacitors: cylindrical capacitor
  • disc capacitor
  • ceramic capacitor
  • capacitor array
  • electrolytic capacitor
  • tantalum capacitors 1.5 Transformer: 6-0-6
  • 12-0-12, 9V, 12V transformers. 1.6 Electronic components: Relay
  • Zener diode, LED, Photo diode, BJT, Photo transistor, LDR, Solar cell
  • Opto- coupler. 1.7 Testing of Resistor
  • Diode and Transistor
  • Inductor using multimeter. 1.8 Electronic Workshop Tools: Bread board
  • Solder iron
  • solder- stand
  • screwdriver set, wire stripper
  • de-solder pump
  • De-solder wick
  • drilling machine

Unit 2 Electrical Wiring & Electrical Safety Devices

7 lecture hours

  • 2.1 Cable and Wire: Classification of power cables
  • armored and unarmored
  • Classification of wire as per insulation code and color code
  • Standard Wire Gauge
  • 3-core, and 4- core. 2.2 Electrical Safety: Fuse, MCB, ELCB, and RCCB. 2.3 Earthling: Pipe and Plate Earthing. 2.4 Domestic wiring: staircase wiring
  • Sample example of one room electrification. 2.5 Main Board Wiring: wiring of main board using fuse
  • MCB and ELCB/RCCB

Unit 3 Electrical Testing Devices and Precautionary system

6 lecture hours

  • 3.1 2-Pole voltage tester: Test voltage and continuity using 2-Pole voltage tester. 3.2 Megger: Test insulator resistance of 1-phase and 3-phase windings
  • Program Name: Engineering Level: Diploma Branch: Power Electronics Engineering Course / Subject Code : DI01024011 Course / Subject Name : Electrical and Electronics Workshop 3.3 Precautionary steps: 3.4 Use of Protective Equipment and tools: Insulating mate
  • Hand gloves
  • arc flash suit
  • electric safety shoes
  • arc flash boundaries
  • rescue hook. 3.5 Adopt safe work practices: Job Hazard Analysis for electric equipment and wiring system
  • SOP for electric equipment and wiring system

Unit 4 Printed Circuit Board (PCB)

4 lecture hours

  • 4.1 PCB layout. 4.2 PCB design software. 4.3 PCB layout - Component side and copper side. 4.4 Tracing for PCB Fabrication. 4.5 Soldering - types - selection of materials and soldering practice in connectors and general purpose PCB

Unit 5 Cells and batteries

5 lecture hours

  • 5.1 Classification: Primary Cells
  • rechargeable batteries
  • Reserve Batteries
  • Fuel Cells. 5.2 IEC standard of batteries
  • Battery capacity. 5.3 Battery Technology used for EVs: Ni-MH
