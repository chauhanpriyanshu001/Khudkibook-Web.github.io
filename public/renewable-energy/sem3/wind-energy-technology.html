Wind Energy Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Wind Energy Technology (subject code DI03064011) as prescribed for RE · Semester 3 divides the course into 5 units, covering 59 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Basic Introduction of Wind Energy Contents

8 lecture hours

  • Historic Background and Merits and Demerits (2) Nature & Origin of Wind
  • Variation of wind speed with height (3) Properties of Air & Wind and Wind Characteristics (4) Criteria for on shore & off shore site selection of wind plant (5) Estimation of Wind energy at site (6) Wind Energy Conversion System and Application (7) Environmental aspects (8) Comparison with other Energy renewable sources (9) Wind energy scenario and programs in India
