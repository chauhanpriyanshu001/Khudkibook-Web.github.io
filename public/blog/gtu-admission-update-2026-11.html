How GTU Admissions Work

  1. Admissions run through the ACAC portal (formerly GCAS). Registration, choice listing and acceptance all happen there.
  2. Merit lists are published in rounds. Each round shortlists candidates from the choices already listed, and you accept the allotment within a short window.
  3. After allotment comes document verification at the allotted college, then fee payment — failing to pay within the window sends the seat back to the pool.
Once ACAS is live, GTU conducts the entire process online. GTU does not ask for money by hand, by UPI to a personal ID, or through a "agent". Every fee is paid on the portal.

Keep These Dates Somewhere You Will See Them

  • Round start and last date to register / list choices.
  • Merit list publication date.
  • Accept-allotment last date (usually only a few days).
  • Document verification and fee payment window at the college.
