Applied Physics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Applied Physics (subject code C4300004) as prescribed for RE · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 44 listed topics. This curriculum applies from the Jul-2023 intake. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

  • 1.1. Measurement and units in engineering and science
