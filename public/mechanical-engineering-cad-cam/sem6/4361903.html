Design of Machine Elements Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Design of Machine Elements (subject code 4361903) as prescribed for ME( · Semester 6 divides the course into 7 units, covering 28 listed topics. This curriculum applies from the 1/1/2025 12:00:00 AM intake. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Introduction. 1.a List various factors to be considered for design process. 1.b Identify and select materials that can be used for design...

  • 1.1. General consideration and factors influencing the design of machine elements and design process.
  • 1.2. Various materials used in manufacturing of machine elements and their properties.
  • 1.3. Types of loads, types of stresses, concept of stress concentration and factor of safety.
  • 1.4. Standardization and preferred numbers, numeric examples on preferred numbers.

Unit 2 Design of machine elements subjected to direct stresses. 2.a Describe the design process of simple elements like linkages, etc.

  • 2.1. Illustration of simple machine elements subjected to direct stresses- independently and identification of resisting areas (simple numeric examples).
  • 2.2. Design of simple machine elements subjected to uni-axial direct stresses- independently.
  • 2.3. Design procedure (with numeric examples), steps, identification of resisting areas and design of: i. Knuckle joint ii. Cotter joint. iii. Riveted joints. Design of Machine Elements Course Code: 4361903

Unit 3 Design of machine elements subjected to bending stresses. 3.a State the fundamental bending equation. 3.b State modulus of various sections

  • 3.1. Principle of bending and its fundamental equation.
  • 3.2. Modulus of various sections, example of pure bending like levers, beams, axle, etc.
  • 3.3. Types of levers.
  • 3.4. Design procedure (with numeric example) of levers including cross section of arms, bosses and pins.
  • 3.5. Design procedure (with numeric example) of leaf spring.

Unit 4 Design of machine elements subjected to direct and twisting moments. 4.a State fundamental equation of twisting

  • 4.1. Fundamental equation of twisting moment with design procedure.
  • 4.2. Types of shafts with important features of each.
  • 4.3. Design of shafts (with numeric examples).
  • 4.4. Types of keys, applications of each and design procedure (with numeric examples).
  • 4.5. Types of couplings and applications.
  • 4.6. Design of muff and flange couplings (with numeric examples).
  • 4.7. Types of spring, terminology related to helical spring and applications of helical spring. the design procedure of machine elements subjected to twisting moment.

Unit 5 Design of machine elements subjected to direct and bending stresses. 5.a Define eccentric loading. 5.b Draw frame-clamp,

  • 5.1. Eccentric loading- i. Concept, ii. Illustrations like frame, C-clamp, Bracket, Column of drilling machine etc. iii. Design of machine element like C- Clamp, bracket, Column of drilling machine. (with numeric examples).

Unit 6 Design of pressure vessels. 6.a Define pressure vessels 6.b State types of pressure vessels with range of pressure. 6.c Design simple thi...

  • 6.1. Types and applications of pressure vessels used in industries. State Range of pressure also.
  • 6.2. Design of thick and thin cylinders (with numeric examples).
  • 6.3. Design of thin spherical shell (with numeric examples). Design of Machine Elements Course Code: 4361903

Unit 7 Selection procedure for bearings. 7.a Classify bearings. 7.b Explain designation of bearings. 7.c Select appropriate anti- friction beari...

  • 7.1. Classification of bearings.
  • 7.2. Bearing designation as per IS.
  • 7.3. Antifriction bearings: types, advantages, applications.
  • 7.4. Selection procedure of anti-friction bearings.
  • 7.5. Calculation for anti-friction bearings: basic dynamic load, load rating, equivalent load, bearing life.
