Automobile Engines Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Automobile Engines (subject code BE04002011) as prescribed for Auto · Semester 4 divides the course into 10 units, covering 58 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE04002011)

Unit 1 Engine Construction and Operation

5 lecture hours

  • Constructional details of 4-stroke engines
  • Working principle
  • Otto and Diesel cycle
  • Valve timing diagrams
  • Actual indicator diagram
  • Fuels (Petrol & Diesel)
  • Ignition quality
  • Octane & Cetane numbers

Unit 2 Two-Stroke Engines

3 lecture hours

  • Construction and operation
  • Comparison with four-stroke
  • Port timing diagrams
  • Firing order and its significance

Unit 3 SI Engine Fuel System

6 lecture hours

  • Carburetor working principle
  • Circuits (starting
  • maximum power)
  • Constant choke/vacuum carburetors
  • Fuel feed systems (mechanical/electrical pumps)
  • Petrol injection
  • LPG & CNG fuel systems
  • MPFI systems

Unit 4 CI Engine Fuel System

6 lecture hours

  • Requirements of injection system
  • Air & solid injection
  • Jerk & distributor type pumps
  • Pressure waves
  • Injection lag
  • Unit injector
  • Mechanical & pneumatic governors
  • Fuel injectors & nozzle types
  • Spray characteristics
  • Injection timing
  • Pump calibration
  • CRDI systems

Unit 5 Cooling & Lubrication Systems

4 lecture hours

  • Need for cooling
  • Liquid cooled
  • Pressure cooling systems
  • Mist lubrication
  • Wet sump
  • Dry sump
  • Properties of lubricants & coolants

Unit 6 Combustion & Combustion Chambers

6 lecture hours

  • Combustion in SI & CI engines
  • Stages of combustion
  • Flame propagation
  • Pressure rise
  • Abnormal combustion
  • Effect of engine variables
  • Combustion chamber types & design factors

Unit 7 Scavenging

2 lecture hours

  • Theoretical methods
  • Scavenging pumps

Unit 8 Supercharging & Turbocharging

4 lecture hours

  • Charge cooling
  • Types & relative merits
  • Matching of turbochargers

Unit 9 Engine Testing & Performance

  • Automotive and stationary tests
  • Engine power & efficiencies. 4 Program Name: Bachelor of Engineering Level: UG Branch: Automobile Engineering Subject Code: BE04002011 Subject Name: Automobile Engines

Unit 10 Performance Characteristics

5 lecture hours

  • Variables affecting performance
  • Methods to improve performance
  • Heat balance
  • Performance maps
  • Drivability diagnosis
