How to Download GTU Textbooks Free on Khudkibook (2026 Guide)

19 September 2026 4 min read Guides, GTU

Scrolling through five different websites at 11 PM to find one textbook PDF is something every diploma student has done at least once. This guide shows the fastest way to find and download GTU study material on Khudkibook — textbooks (English and Gujarati), official syllabus, and previous year question papers — in under a minute.

Step 1 — Find Your Branch

From the homepage, choose your Diploma or Degree (BE) section, then your branch — for example Computer Engineering, Civil, Electrical, or Mechanical. Every branch page lists all of its semesters in order.

Tip: you do not need to remember the full branch name. The search bar at the top of every page works with the subject name or its subject code (for example 3300001).

Step 2 — Open Your Semester

Tap your semester (Sem 1 through Sem 6, or higher semesters for BE). Each semester page shows every subject that appears in the official scheme for that semester — including common subjects like Mathematics, Physics, and English.

Step 3 — Pick Your Subject Page

Each subject page gives you buttons for:

  • Book — opens the textbook links (English book first, then Gujarati medium where available, then chapter-wise PDFs).
  • Syllabus — the official GTU curriculum PDF for that subject code.
  • Paper — previous year question papers, organised by exam session (Winter / Summer year by year).

Step 4 — Download

Click the button, then tap the PDF link you need. The file opens in a new tab and downloads in one tap. That's it — no login, no OTP, no "complete a survey to unlock".

If a subject's book button says "Coming Soon", the textbook isn't available yet. You can still get its syllabus and papers, and you can request the book — the most-requested subjects are added first.

Downloading On Mobile

Khudkibook works fully in your phone browser. If you download a lot of material, the Khudkibook Android app also lets you read offline and bookmark favourite subjects.

Quick Checklist

  1. Homepage → choose Diploma or Degree.
  2. Select branch → select semester.
  3. Open the subject page → tap Book / Syllabus / Paper.
  4. Tap the PDF → it downloads, free.
Save time during exam week: bookmark your branch's semester index page once, and everything is two taps away forever.
