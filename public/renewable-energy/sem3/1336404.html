Fundamental of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Fundamental of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer (subject code 1336404) as prescribed for RE · Semester 3 divides the course into 6 units, covering 60 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Fundamentals of Thermodynamics

  • 1.1. Introduction and applications of Engineering thermodynamics.
  • 1.2. Basic thermodynamic Concepts. (a) State, System, Boundary and Surroundings. (b) Types of Systems and boundaries with examples. (c) Thermodynamic properties, theirunits and classifications. (d) Energy, Heat, Work, Power and its simple numerical. levels) Topics and Sub-topics (e) Thermodynamic equilibrium. (f) Thermodynamic Process and Cycle.
  • 1.3. Zeroth law of thermodynamics and Its application. Fundamental of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer Course Code: 1336404 Page 3 of11

Unit 2 First Law of Thermodynamics

  • 2.1. Statement of the first law of thermodynamics. (a) Law of conservation of energy. (b) Heat and work relation with Joule’s Experiment.
  • 2.2. Application of the first law of thermodynamics: a) Open system (Flow Processes). b) Closed system (Non-flow Processes). c) Definition of the flow process, control volume and flow work. d) Steady and unsteady flow processes. e) Steady Flow Energy Equations (SFEE) and its applications in Nozzle, Diffuser, Boiler, Turbine, Compressor, Condenser, and t
  • 2.3. Simple numerical examples based on the above.
  • 2.4. Identify the applications of First law of thermodynamics for green environment.

Unit 3 Second Law of Thermodynamics

  • 3.1. Limitations of the first law of thermodynamics.
  • 3.2. Concept of heat source, heat sink, heat engine, heat pump, refrigerator and simple numerical on thermal efficiency and COP (Coefficient of Performance) respectively.
  • 3.3. Statement of the second law of thermodynamics: a) Kelvin Planck Statement b) Clausius Statement
  • 3.4. Applications of the second law of thermodynamics. Also identify its applications for green environment. Fundamental of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer Course Code: 1336404 Page 4 of11 levels) Topics and Sub-topics
  • 3.5. Concept of reversibility and irreversibility. List of irreversibility only.
  • 3.6. Definition of Entropy and its T-ds equation. (Without Derivations)
  • 3.7. Statement of the third law of thermodynamics.

Unit 4 Ideal Gases and Thermodynamic Processes

  • 4.1. Concept of Ideal gas. Boyle’s law, Charle’s law and Gay-Lussac law for ideal gases.
  • 4.2. Thermodynamic Processes, its representation on P-V (Pressure- Volume) and T-S (Temperature-Entropy) diagram: a) Constant Volume Process b) Constant Pressure Process c) Constant Temperature Process d) Adiabatic Process e) Polytropic Process f) Throttling Process
  • 4.3. Equations of P-V-T relationship, work transfer, heat transfer and internal energy of the above processes. (Without derivations)
  • 4.4. Simple numerical based on the above.

Unit 5 Thermodynamic Cycles

  • 5.1. Classifications of thermodynamic cycle.
  • 5.2. Carnot cycle and its representation on P-v and T-s diagram.
  • 5.3. Thermal efficiency of Carnot cycle and its simple numerical (without derivation).
  • 5.4. Concept of air standard efficiency.
  • 5.4. Otto, Diesel, Dual and Brayton cycle (Without derivation) representation on P-v & T-s diagram. Equation of air standard efficiency and simple examples.
  • 5.6. Representation of Reversed Carnot cycle and Reversed Brayton cycle on P-V and T-s diagram respectively. Fundamental of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer Course Code: 1336404 Page 5 of11 levels) Topics and Sub-topics

Unit 6 Heat Transfer

  • 5.1. Concept of conduction, convection, and radiation.
  • 5.2. Heat exchanger: a) Concept, classification, and application b) Logarithmic Mean Temperature Difference (LMTD) (No derivation)
  • 5.3. Simple numerical examples. a.
