Fluid Flow Operation Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Fluid Flow Operation (subject code 2130502) as prescribed for Chemical · Semester 3 divides the course into 10 units, covering 91 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2130502)

Unit 1 Fluid static and its application

40 lecture hours

  • Nature of fluids
  • Pressure concept
  • Hydrostatic equilibrium
  • decanters like continuous gravity
  • centrifugal etc. 02

Unit 2 Fluid Flow Phenomena

5 lecture hours

  • Velocity fluid
  • Velocity gradient and rate of shear
  • Newtonian and Non- Newtonian fluids
  • Viscosity and momentum flux
  • Reynolds number and its significance
  • laminar and turbulent flow
  • Reynolds stresses
  • Eddy viscosity
  • Laminar and Turbulent flow in boundary layers
  • boundary layer formation in straight tubes
  • boundary separation and wake formation

Unit 3 Basic equations of Fluid Flow

5 lecture hours

  • Mass velocity
  • average velocity
  • potential flow
  • stream tubes
  • macroscopic momentum balance
  • momentum correction factor
  • Equation of continuity
  • Bernoulli's equation
  • corrections for fluid friction
  • pump work in Bernoulli’s equations
  • angular momentum equations

Unit 4 Flow of incompressible fluids in Conduits and Thin Layers

  • Flow of incompressible fluids in Conduits and Thin Layers in pipes
  • relation between skin friction and wall shear
  • friction factor laminar flow in pipes
  • kinetic energy correction factor and momentum correction factor for laminar flow of Newtonian fluids
  • Hagen-Poiseuille equation
  • effect of roughness
  • friction factor chart
  • friction factor inflow through channels of non-circular cross section
  • equivalent diameter
  • hydraulic radius
  • friction from changes in velocity or direction
  • flow through sudden enlargement of cross section
  • flow through sudden contraction of cross section
  • effect of fittings and valves
  • form friction losses in 07 Bernoulli’s equations
  • separation of boundary layers in diverging channel

Unit 5 Flow of Compressible fluids

60 lecture hours

  • Mach number
  • continuity equation total energy balance equation
  • velocity of sound
  • processes of compressible of flow like isentropic expansion
  • adiabatic frictional flow
  • isothermal frictional flow
  • velocity in nozzles. 04

Unit 6 Flow past immersed bodies

4 lecture hours

  • Introduction to Drag
  • drag coefficient
  • form drag, and stream lining
  • friction in fluids through bed of solids
  • condition of fluidization
  • types of fluidization
  • application of fluidization
  • continuous fluidization
  • slurry and pneumatic transport

Unit 7 Transportation and Metering of fluid

6 lecture hours

  • Pipe and tubing
  • joint and fittings selection of pipe sizes
  • prevention of leakage around moving parts
  • stuffing boxes
  • mechanical seals
  • valves like Gate
  • Plug cocks
  • Check valves

Unit 8 Fluid moving machinery

8 lecture hours

  • Pumps its characteristics like developed head power requirement suction lift and cavitations
  • positive displacement pumps like reciprocating
  • rotary pumps
  • centrifugal pumps and its theory
  • characteristic of head capacity relation
  • pump priming
  • blowers like positive displacement
  • centrifugal blowers
  • compressor efficiency
  • vacuum pumps
  • jet ejectors
  • comparison of devices for moving fluids

Unit 9 Measurement of flowing fluids

8 lecture hours

  • Full bore meter like venturimeter
  • orifice meter
  • coefficient of discharge of venturimeter
  • area meters like Rotameter
  • target meters
  • vortex-shedding meters
  • coriolis meters
  • magnetic meters etc.
  • insertion meters like pitot tubes etc
  • Recent advancement in different pumps
  • valves and measuring devices

Unit 10 Dimensional Analysis

5 lecture hours

  • Different methods of dimensional analysis applied to fluid flow problems
