Contribute to Khudkibook

Share your GTU study material with thousands of students. All contributions are submitted and managed through the Khudkibook app.

How It Works

1

Download Khudkibook App

Install the Khudkibook app on your Android/iOS device to get started.

2

Sign In

Log into your Khudkibook account in the app. A verified email helps speed up the process.

3

Go to Contribute

Open the Contribute section in the app. Select your branch, semester, and the subject you want to upload for.

4

Upload Your Material

Choose the material type: Notes, Practical/Lab Record, PYQ, or Paper Solution. Add a title, year (if applicable), description, and upload your PDF file (maximum 20MB).

5

Review & Approval

Your submission goes for review. Once approved, it becomes visible on the subject page and you earn points for your contribution. You can track status in the app.

What to Upload

  • Lecture notes
  • Lab manuals & practical files
  • Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
  • Paper solutions

Guidelines

  • Upload only PDF files
  • Maximum file size: 20MB
  • Upload original or permitted material only
  • Respect copyright and university guidelines

Benefits

  • Help fellow GTU students
  • Earn points on approval
  • Get badges for milestones
  • Redeem rewards in the app
