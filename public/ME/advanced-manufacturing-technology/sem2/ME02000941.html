Flexible Manufacturing System Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Flexible Manufacturing System (subject code ME02000941) as prescribed for AMT · Semester 2 divides the course into 7 units, covering 29 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME02000941)

Unit 1 Introduction

8 lecture hours

  • Mathematical Models of Production Performance: Production rate
  • Production capacity
  • Utilisation and availability
  • Lead time, and Work Program Name: Master of Engineering Level: PG Subject Code: ME02000941 Subject Name: Flexible Manufacturing System in progress
  • Economic Differentiation and justification of traditional manufacturing to flexible manufacturing system
  • Concept of FMS, Types and Components of FMS, Integration in Data Processing Systems
  • Examples of FMS installations

Unit 2 Group Technology

4 lecture hours

  • part families
  • algorithms and models for G.T. - Rank order clustering
  • Bond energy
  • mathematical model for machine – component cell formation
  • Design and manufacturing attributes
  • Parts classification and coding
  • concept of composite job machine group
  • cell group tooling
  • design rationalization

Unit 3 Computer Aided Process Planning

4 lecture hours

  • Traditional Process planning
  • Data required for Process Planning
  • computer Aided Process Planning types
  • Backward and Forward approach
  • feature based CAPP and CAD based CAPP

Unit 4 Distributed data processing in FMS

8 lecture hours

  • DBMS and their applications in CAD/CAM and FMS, Distributed systems in FMS, Integration of CAD and CAM, Part programming in FMS, tool data base
  • Clamping devices and fixtures data base

Unit 5 Interfacing of computers - Machine tool controllers and handling systems

6 lecture hours

  • communications standards - programmable Logic Controllers (PLC's)
  • Computer aided Project planning
  • dynamic part scheduling

Unit 6 Material Transport system and Storage

  • Industrial robots in material handling

Unit 7 FMS Planning and Implementation issues, Quantitative analysis of FMS

  • Bottleneck model
  • Extended Bottleneck model and sizing the FMS. 08 14
