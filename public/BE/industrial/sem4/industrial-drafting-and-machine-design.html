Industrial Drafting and Machine Design Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Industrial Drafting and Machine Design (subject code 3142508) as prescribed for Industrial · Semester 4 divides the course into 12 units, covering 103 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3142508)

Unit 1 Basics of stress and strain

5 lecture hours

  • 3 -D state of stress (Concept only) Normal/axial stresses: Tensile & compressive Stresses: Shear and complementary shear Strains: Linear
  • thermal and volumetric
  • Hooke’s law, Elastic Constants: Modulus of elasticity
  • Poisson’s ratio
  • Modulus of rigidity and bulk modulus and relations between them with derivation

Unit 2 Moment of inertia of planar cross -sections

  • Derivation of equation of moment of inertia of standard lamina using first principle
  • Parallel & perpendicular axes theorems
  • polar moment of inertia

Unit 3 Flexural stresses – Theory of simple bending, Assumptions, derivation of equation of

3 lecture hours

  • neutral axis
  • determination of bending stresses
  • section modulus of rectangular & circular (solid & hollow), I, T, Angle
  • channel sections

Unit 4 Torsion

3 lecture hours

  • Derivation of equation of torsion
  • application of theory of torsion equation to solid & hollow circular shaft
  • torsional rigidity

Unit 5 Introduction to Machine Design

5 lecture hours

  • Design procedure
  • Selection of preferred sizes
  • Aesthetic and Ergonomic considerations in Design
  • Manufacturing considerations in Design
  • Mechanical Properties of Materials
  • Effect of Alloying elements and heat treatment on properties of steels
  • Materials Selection in Machine Design

Unit 6 Design Against Static Load

10 lecture hours

  • Concepts of stresses and Strain
  • Combinations of Axial
  • Torsional and Bending loads
  • Theories of Failures: Distortion energy (von Mises)
  • Maximum-Shear stress
  • Maximum Principal stress
  • Selection and Use of theories of failures
  • Factor of safety
  • Contact stresses
  • Crushing and Bearing stress
  • Application Problems: Eccentric Loading
  • Cotter and Knuckle Joints
  • Design and analysis of levers: Cranked
  • Bell crank
  • Rocker arm

Unit 7 Beams and Columns

  • Different types of supports / end conditions
  • Revision of Stresses in beams: Effect of Section
  • Orientation, and type of loading
  • Deflection of beams for different loading conditions
  • Compressive axial loading of columns and struts
  • 05 Subject Code: 3142508 w.e.f
  • AY 2018-19 Slenderness ratio
  • Compressive stress and Buckling of members
  • Effect of end conditions
  • Euler’s Formula
  • validity and limitations
  • Rankine’s Formula
  • stresses in curved beam

Unit 8 Shafts, Keys and Couplings

8 lecture hours

  • Design of solid and hollow circular shaft subjected to torque and combined loading for rigidity and stiffness
  • Design of Keys and splines
  • Design of Couplings: Concept of rigid and flexible couplings
  • Design of: Clamp
  • Rigid flange and Flexible couplings

Unit 9 Power Screws and Threaded Joints

7 lecture hours

  • Forms of thread
  • Single and Multiple threaded screw
  • Terminology of power screw
  • Torque requirement of lifting/lowering
  • Self- locking
  • Efficiency of threads
  • coefficient of friction
  • design of screw and nut
  • Basic types of screw fastening
  • Cap and Set screw
  • Bolt of Uniform strength
  • locking devices
  • Terminology of Screw thread
  • Bolted Joint: Simple and Eccentric loading
  • Torque requirement for bolt tightening

Unit 10 Springs

5 lecture hours

  • Types of springs
  • Styles of ends
  • Stress and deflection equations
  • Series and parallel connections
  • Spring materials
  • Design of helical springs
  • Concentric springs
  • Helical torsion spring
  • Multi leaf spring
  • Nipping of leaf springs

Unit 11 Intersection of Surfaces

3 lecture hours

  • Line of intersection
  • Intersection of two prisms
  • Intersection of two cylinders
  • Intersection of prism and cylinder
  • Intersection of cone
  • Intersection of cone and prism
  • Intersection of cone and cone
  • Intersection of sphere and cylinder or prism

Unit 12 Elements of Production Drawing Tolerances, Limits and Fits

4 lecture hours

  • Basic Definitions
  • Maximum Metal Condition
  • Least Metal Condition
  • Grade of tolerance
  • Linear and Angular Tolerances
  • Fundamental deviations
  • Types of Fits and its basis
  • Gauge design
  • Basic terminology of GD & T, Different tolerance characteristics
  • symbols and tolerance modifiers
  • Different aspects of datums
  • Parameters of surface texture and qualifications
  • Relation of surface roughness and various manufacturing processes
  • Surface Lay Indication
  • Discussion related to various Indian standards/conventions used in design and drawings
