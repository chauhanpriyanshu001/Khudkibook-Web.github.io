Repair and Rehabilitation of Buildings Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Repair and Rehabilitation of Buildings (subject code 2184011) as prescribed for Civil Infra · Semester 8 divides the course into 6 units, covering 92 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2184011)

Unit 1 Introduction

4 lecture hours

  • Introduction of health monitoring of civil structures
  • Need and objectives of condition assessment of structures
  • Overview of various evaluation techniques for evaluating performance of structures
  • Introduction of performance indices or rating system of the structures

Unit 2 Durability and serviceability of structures

6 lecture hours

  • Construction materials characteristics and properties
  • Quality assurance criterion of construction materials and construction practices
  • Factors influencing durability and serviceability of the structures
  • Stages of distress in structures

  • Condition Survey: Preliminary Inspection
  • Planning of condition assessment of the structure

  • Field/Laboratory Testing
  • damage rating system of the structures

  • Pullout test
  • Core sampling and testing
  • Chemical Tests: Carbonation test
  • Chloride content

  • Impact-echo test
  • Dynamic Testing of Structures Present condition of structures: Rating system of the structure

Unit 4 Selection of repair materials for concrete

  • Essential parameters for repair materials
  • Criteria for material selection
  • Methodology of selection
  • Health and safety precautions for handling and applications of repair materials Materials for repair: Premixed cement concrete/mortars

  • Polyester resins
  • Surface coatings
  • Grouting materials
  • Bonding agents. 5 12.5%

  • Thin polymer overlays

  • Plate bonding
  • RCC jacketing
  • Propping and supporting
  • Fiber wrap technique
  • Foundation rehabilitation methods
  • Chemical and electro-chemical methods of repair 7 17.5%
