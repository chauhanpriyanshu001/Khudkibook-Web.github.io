Environment and Sustainability Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Environment and Sustainability (subject code 4300003) as prescribed for COMPUTER · Semester 4 divides the course into 5 units, covering 43 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Ecosystem

  • 1.1. Structure and components of ecosystem
  • 1.2. Types of Ecosystem, changes in ecosystem
  • 1.3. Various natural cycles like carbon, Nitrogen, Sulphur, Phosphorus
  • 1.4. Ecosystem conservation, carrying capacity of earth, Biomes in India, (ESA) Ecologically sensitive areas
  • 1.5. Bio diversity, its need and importance, International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) red list
  • 1.6. Concept of Ecological foot print, virtual water, global ecological overshoot

Unit 2 Pollution and its types

  • 2.1. Definition of pollution and pollutant
  • 2.2. Air pollution, classification and its sources
  • 2.3. Air pollution control Equipments
  • 2.4. Water pollution, pollution parameters like BOD,COD, pH, Total suspended solids, Turbidity, Total Solids
  • 2.5. Waste water treatment like primary, Environment and Sustainability Course Code: ................. NITTTR Bhopal – GTU - COGC-2021 Curriculum above level) Topics and Sub-topics
  • 2.6. Solid waste generation, sources and characteristics of Muncipal solid waste
  • 2.7. Collection and disposal of Muncipal waste and Hazardous waste
  • 2.8. Noise pollution- its effects, sources and measurement
  • 2.9. Plastic waste and its hazard
  • 2.10. E waste and its hazard

Unit 3 Renewable sources of energy

  • 3.1. Need of Renewable energy and energy policy
  • 3.2. Solar energy: National solar mission
  • 3.3. Features of solar thermal and PV systems Advanced collector, Solar Pond, Solar water heater, Solar dryer, polycrystalline, monocrystalline and thin film PV systems
  • 3.4. Wind Energy: Growth of wind power in India
  • 3.5. Types of wind turbines – Vertical axis wind turbines (VAWT) and horizontal axis wind turbines (HAWT)
  • 3.6. Types of HAWTs – drag and lift types
  • 3.7. Biomass: Overview of biomass as energy source. Thermal characteristics of biomass as fuel
  • 3.8. Anaerobic digestion, Biogas production mechanism, utilization and storage.
  • 3.9. New energy sources: Geothermal energy, Ocean energy sources, Tidal energy conversion, Hydrogen energy

  • 4.3. Global warming-causes, effect, process
  • 4.4. Greenhouse effect
  • 4.5. Ozone depletion
  • 4.6. Factors affecting climate change
  • 4.7. Impact and mitigation

  • 5.1. Environmental policies in India
  • 5.2. Air act, water act, Environment protection act, wild life protection act, Forest conservation act, Biodiversity act
  • 5.3. Environmental management system: ISO 14000, definition and benefits
