The official GTU curriculum for Tribology of Elastomers (subject code ME01088081) as prescribed for RE · Semester 1 divides the course into 6 units, covering 25 listed topics. 9 lecture hours

7 lecture hours

  • Definition and Essential Characters

Unit 4 Theory of Rubber Abrasion

8 lecture hours

  • Dry Abrasion
  • Oily Abrasion
  • Wet Abrasion.

8 lecture hours
