Human Anatomy and Physiology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Human Anatomy and Physiology (subject code 2130304) as prescribed for Biomed · Semester 3 divides the course into 9 units, covering 64 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2130304)

Unit 1 Introduction to the human body, physiology of human cell & tissues

4 lecture hours

  • Definition of anatomy and physiology
  • Terms related to anatomy and physiology
  • Anatomical planes and positions
  • Structural Organization
  • The internal environment and homeostasis
  • Structure And Function of Cell & Cell organelles
  • Elementary tissues of the body: epithelial
  • connective and nervous

Unit 2 Body Fluid (Blood)

4 lecture hours

  • Composition of Blood
  • Functions of Blood
  • Cellular Content of Blood: their properties and functions
  • Blood Groups
  • Hemoglobin and its estimation

Unit 3 Respiratory System

5 lecture hours

  • Structure and function of various Organs of respiratory system
  • Mechanism of respiration
  • Muscles of respiration
  • Control of respiration Physiological variables affect ingrespiration
  • Physical principle of gas exchange
  • pulmonary volumes and capacities

Unit 4 Cardiovascular System

5 lecture hours

  • External features and structure of heart
  • Blood vessels
  • Physiology and properties of cardiac muscle
  • Cardiac cycle
  • Cardiac output
  • Heart rate
  • Heart Sound, and Introduction to ECG, Blood pressure and blood pressure control

Unit 5 Nervous System

5 lecture hours

  • Synapse and neurotransmitters
  • Central and Peripheral nervous system
  • various parts of nervous system
  • Brain: Parts and functions
  • Spinal cord, CSF, Ventricles of the brain
  • Autonomic nervous system
  • Reflex action

Unit 6 Special Senses

5 lecture hours

  • Eye: Anatomy of Eye & Physiology of Vision
  • Ear: Structure of Ear & Physiology of Hearing
  • Nose: Sense of Smell
  • Tongue: Sense of Taste
  • Skin: Structure & Functions of skin

Unit 7 Digestive System

5 lecture hours

  • Various organs of digestive system
  • movement of gastrointestinal tract
  • physiological activities in mouth
  • gall bladder
  • small and large Intestine
  • Digestion and absorption

Unit 8 Excretory System

4 lecture hours

  • Anatomy of Urinary System
  • Physiology of urine formation
  • physiology of micturition
  • Concentration and Dilution of urine
  • Composition of Urine

Unit 9 Skeletal and muscular System

42 lecture hours

  • Bone: Types
  • Functions Structure and Development of bone
  • Axial Skeleton: Skull
  • Cranial Cavity
  • Vertebral Column
  • Thoracic Cage Joints: Types of joints
  • Main synovial joints of the limbs Muscular System: Classification of muscles
  • Anatomy and Physiology of skeletal muscle
  • Muscle of body
  • Physiology of muscle contraction
  • Structure and physiology of smooth muscle
  • cardiac muscle
  • difference of cardiac muscle
  • smooth and cardiac muscle
  • Anatomy and Physiology of neuromuscular junction. 5 10 Total: Total
