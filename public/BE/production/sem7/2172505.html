Supply Chain Management Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Supply Chain Management (subject code 2172505) as prescribed for Production · Semester 7 divides the course into 6 units, covering 24 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2172505)

Unit 1 Introduction

2 lecture hours

  • Generic Types of supply chain
  • Various Definitions and Implications
  • Major Drivers of Supply chain

Unit 2 Strategic Decisions- in Supply Chain Management

5 lecture hours

  • Business Strategy
  • Core Competencies in Supply Chain
  • Strategic SC Decisions
  • Customer Reletationship Management Strategy
  • Supplier Relationship Management Strategy

Unit 3 Source of Management in Supply Chain

4 lecture hours

  • Elements of Strategic Sourcing
  • A Collaborative Perspective
  • Development of Partnership

Unit 4 Inventory Management in Supply Chain

11 lecture hours

  • Types of Inventory
  • Supply/ Demand Uncertainties
  • Inventory costs
  • Selective Inventory Control
  • Vendor Manage Inventory system
  • Inventory Performance Measure 5

Unit 5 Logistics In Supply Chain Management

5 lecture hours

  • Transportation Selection
  • Models for Transportation and Distribution
  • Third Party Logistics
  • Overview of Indian Infrastructure for Transportation

Unit 6 Information Technology in Supply Chain

4 lecture hours

  • Types of IT Solutions like Electronic Data Inter change (EDI)
  • Intranet/ Extranet
  • Data Mining/ Data Warehousing
