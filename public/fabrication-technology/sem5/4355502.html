The official GTU curriculum for Process Piping Fabrication (subject code 4355502) as prescribed for FT · Semester 5 divides the course into 5 units, covering 72 listed topics.   • 1.13. Pipe sizes & specification

  • 3.6. Calculate dimension of pipe fittings from piping isometric drawing.
  • 3.7. Pipe to pipe joint development (L-joint, T-joint etc.)
