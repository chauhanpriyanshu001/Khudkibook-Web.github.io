Industrial Safety Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Industrial Safety (subject code 3730006) as prescribed for EVT · Semester 3 divides the course into 5 units, covering 61 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3730006)

Unit 1 Unit-I

10 lecture hours

  • Industrial Safety: Accident
  • results and control
  • mechanical and electrical hazards
  • causes and preventive steps/procedure
  • describe salient points of factories act 1948 for health and safety
  • wash rooms
  • drinking water layouts
  • pressure vessels
  • Safety color codes
  • Fire prevention and firefighting
  • equipment and methods

Unit 2 Unit-II

6 lecture hours

  • Fundamentals of Maintenance Engineering: Definition and aim of maintenance engineering
  • Primary and secondary functions and responsibility of maintenance department
  • Types of maintenance
  • Types and applications of tools used for maintenance
  • Maintenance cost & its relation with replacement economy
  • Service life of equipment

Unit 3 Unit-III

10 lecture hours

  • Wear and Corrosion and their Prevention: Wear- types
  • wear reduction methods
  • lubricants-types and Subject Code: 3730006 Industrial Safety Semester – III Subject Name: applications
  • Lubrication methods
  • general sketch
  • working and applications, i
  • Screw down grease cup, ii
  • Pressure grease gun, iii
  • Splash lubrication, iv
  • Gravity lubrication, v
  • Wick feed lubrication vi
  • Side feed lubrication, vii
  • Ring lubrication
  • principle and factors affecting the corrosion
  • Types of corrosion
  • corrosion prevention methods

Unit 4 Unit-IV

12 lecture hours

  • Fault Tracing: Fault tracing-concept and importance
  • decision tree concept
  • need and applications
  • sequence of fault finding activities
  • show as decision tree
  • draw decision tree for problems in machine tools
  • thermal and electrical equipment’s like, i
  • Any one machine tool, ii
  • Pump iii
  • Air compressor, iv
  • Internal combustion engine, v
  • Boiler, vi
  • Electrical motors
  • Types of faults in machine tools and their general causes

Unit 5 Unit-V

6 lecture hours

  • Periodic and Preventive Maintenance: Periodic inspection- concept and need
  • cleaning and repairing schemes
  • overhauling of mechanical components
  • overhauling of electrical motor
  • common troubles and remedies of electric motor
  • repair complexities and its use, definition
  • steps and advantages of preventive maintenance
  • Steps/procedure for periodic and preventive maintenance of: i. tools, ii
  • Pumps, iii
  • Air compressors, iv
  • Diesel generating (DG) sets
  • Program and schedule of preventive maintenance of mechanical and electrical equipment
  • advantages of preventive maintenance
  • Repair cycle concept and importance
