Electric & Hybrid Vehicles Technolog Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Electric & Hybrid Vehicles Technolog (subject code ME01000441) as prescribed for M(&A · Semester 1 divides the course into 4 units, covering 27 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01000441)

Unit 1 Electric and Hybrid Electric Vehicles

10 lecture hours

  • Benefits of EV, Configuration of Electric Vehicles
  • Performance of Electric Vehicles
  • Introduction of Different source of power for Electric Vehicle
  • Tractive effort and Transmission requirement
  • Vehicle performance
  • Tractive effort in normal driving
  • Tractive effort in Gradient
  • Tractive effort for other forces

Unit 2 Energy storage for EV and HEV

10 lecture hours

  • Energy storage requirements
  • Battery parameters
  • Types of Batteries
  • Charging method of battery
  • Fuel Cell basic principle and operation
  • Types of Fuel Cells
  • PEMFC and its operation
  • Modeling of PEMFC
  • Super Capacitors
  • Introduction to Ultra fly wheel
  • Energy management and their strategies used in hybrid vehicle

Unit 3 Electric Vehicle Motors

10 lecture hours

  • DC Motor
  • Induction Motor
  • BLDC Motor and Switch Reluctance Motor – Types
  • Construction operating Principle
  • Equivalent circuit for each motor and selection of motor for vehicle application

Unit 4 Hybrid Electrical Vehicle Subsystem and Configuration

12 lecture hours

  • Series HEV, Parallel HEV, and Series Parallel HEV, Complex HEV system and Its Configuration
  • HEV mode of operation
  • Drive train comparison
