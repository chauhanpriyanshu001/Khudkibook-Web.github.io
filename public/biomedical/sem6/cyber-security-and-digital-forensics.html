Cyber Security and Digital Forensics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Cyber Security and Digital Forensics (subject code 4361601) as prescribed for Biomed · Semester 6 divides the course into 5 units, covering 36 listed topics. Unit 1 Introduction of Information Security and Cryptography

  • 1.1. Basic Concept of Information Security
  • 1.2. CIA Triad
  • 1.3. OSI Security Architecture (Security Services, Mechanisms and Attacks)
  • 1.4. Private & Public Key Cryptography
  • 1.5. Message Digest 5 Hashing & SHA

Unit 2 Network and System security

  • 2.1. Types of attacks
  • 2.2. Digital signatures: Definition and Properties
  • 2.3. Pretty Good Privacy (PGP)(brief)
  • 2.4. Secure Socket Layer and Transport Layer Security
  • 2.6. HTTPS (Connection initiation & Connection closure)
  • 2.7. Malicious software: Virus and Related Threats (Trojans, Rootkit, Backdoors, keylogger)
  • 2.8. Firewall :Need and Types
  • 2.9. Proxy Server: Need and Types Cyber Security and Digital Forensic Course Code: 4361601

Unit 3 Cyber Crime

  • 3.2. Classification of cyber-crimes 3.2.1. Organization a. Email Bombing b. Salami Attack c. Logic Bomb d. Trojan Horse e. Web Jacking f. Data diddling g. Denial of Service/ Distributed Denial of Service h. Ransomware
  • 3.2.2. Individual a. Cyber bullying b. Cyber stalking c. Cyber defamation d. Phishing e. Cyber fraud and Cyber theft f. Spyware g. Email spoofing h. Man in the middle attack
  • 3.2.3. Society a. Cyber pornography b. Cyber terrorism c. cyber spying d. Social Engineering Attack e. Online gambling
  • 3.2.4. Property a. Credit Card Fraud b. Software Piracy c. Copyright infringement d. Trademarks violations
  • 3.3. Challenges & Prevention of Cyber Crime
  • 3.4. Cyber Law The Information Technology ACT, 2008 OFFENCES Cyber Security and Digital Forensic Course Code: 4361601 • Section 65 • Section 66 • Section 67 Cyber Security and Digital Forensic Course Code: 4361601

Unit 4 Ethical Hacking

  • 4.1. Concept of Hacking Types of Hackers
  • 4.2. Basics of Ethical Hacking
  • 4.3. The terminology of Hacking (Vulnerability, Exploit, 0-Day)
  • 4.4. Five Steps of Hacking (Information Gathering, Scanning, Gaining Access, Maintaining Access, Covering Tracks)
  • 4.5. Information Gathering (Active, Passive)
  • 4.6. Introduction to Kali Linux OS • Configuration of Kali Linux • Basic Commands Kali Linux • Vulnerability Scanning/ Vulnerability
  • 4.7. Port Scanning
  • 4.8. Remote Administration Tool (RAT)
  • 4.9. Protect System from RAT
  • 4.10. What is Sniffing and Mechanism of Sniffing Session Hijacking Cyber Security and Digital Forensic Course Code: 4361601

Unit 5 DIGITAL FORENSICS

  • 5.1. Introduction to Digital Forensics
  • 5.2. Locard's Principle of Exchange in Digital Forensics
  • 5.3. Branches of Digital Forensics • Disk / Memory Forensics • Network Forensics • Database Forensics • Software forensics • Email Forensics • Malware Forensics • Mobile Forensics
  • 5.4. Phases of digital/computer forensics investigation • Identification • Preservation • Analysis • Documentation • Presentation
  • 5.5. Methods to Preserve a Digital Evidence • Drive Imaging • Hash Values • Chain of Custody
  • 5.6. Critical Steps in Preserving Digital Evidence
  • 5.7. Evidence Role of devices as in Digital Forensics Investigations • Computing Devices • Network Devices and Servers • CCTV • Vehicles
