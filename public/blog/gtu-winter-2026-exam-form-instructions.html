How To Fill The GTU Exam Form

  1. Open the GTU Student Portal at student.gtu.ac.in and log in with your enrolment number and password.
  2. Go to the Examination section and select the Exam Form / Exam Registration option for your session.
  3. Choose your subjects, verify the number of credits and the paper codes shown, then submit.
  4. Pay the examination fee through the payment gateway (or SBI Collect, where GTU asks for it) and download the receipt.
  5. Save the acknowledgement. If the form needs correction, there is usually a short re-edit window — check the circular for it.
Pay the exam fee only through the GTU portal. Anyone asking you for money to "confirm" or "reserve" a form is running a scam.

If You Are Appearing As A Remedial Candidate

Remedial candidates are normally allowed only in subjects they have failed, and usually need prior permission from the Controller of Examination. The circulars in this batch cover the form-filling instructions — they do not by themselves grant eligibility, so read the attached PDF and contact your college examination section before you pay any fee.

After You Submit

  • Your hall ticket / admit card appears on the portal once the exam schedule is declared.
  • Watch this page and the live GTU notices list for the exam date circular and the admit-card link.
  • Download your previous year papers in advance so you can target the right units while preparing.
