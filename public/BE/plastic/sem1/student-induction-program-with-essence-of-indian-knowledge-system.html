Student Induction Program with essence of Indian Knowledge System Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Student Induction Program with essence of Indian Knowledge System (subject code BE01000011) as prescribed for Plastic · Semester 1 divides the course into 9 units, covering 31 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE01000011)

Unit 1 Universal Human Values I (UHV I) 10

  • The purpose is to help develop a holistic perspective about life
  • A universal self-reflective methodology of teaching is adopted
  • It opens the space for the student to explore his/her role (value) in all aspects of living – as an individual
  • as a member of a family
  • as a part of the society and as a unit in nature
  • Through this process of self-exploration
  • students are able to discover the values intrinsic in them

Unit 2 Physical Health and Related Activities 24

  • This module is intended to help understand the basic principles to remain healthy and fit and practice them through a healthy routine which includes exercise
  • sports etc

Unit 3 Familiarization of Department/ Branch and Innovation 2

  • This module is for introducing and relating the student to the institution/department/branch
  • how it plays a role in the development of the society
  • the state
  • nation and the world at large and how students can participate in it

Unit 4 Visit to a Local Area 6

  • To relate to the social environment of the educational institution as well as the area in which it is situated through interaction with the people
  • history, and politics

Unit 5 Lectures by Eminent People 2

  • Listening to the life and times of eminent people from various fields like academics
  • industry etc. about careers, art, self-management and so on enriches the student’s perspective and provides a holistic learning experience

Unit 6 Proficiency Modules 4

  • This module is to help fill the gaps in basic competency required for further inputs to be absorbed
  • It includes effort to make students proficient in interpersonal communication and expression as well as awareness about linguistics and thereafter NLP

Unit 7 Literature / Literary Activities including Indian Knowledge

  • System-I (IKS-I) Through the exposure of local
  • national and international literature
  • this module is aimed at helping the student explore by practicing traditional as well as contemporary values and thought

Unit 8 Creative Practices 24

  • This module is to help develop the clarity of humanistic culture and its creative
  • joyful expression through practice of art forms like dance
  • sculpture etc
  • national and international case studies
  • particularly Indian Knowledge Systems
  • Culture and Values

Unit 9 Other Co-curricular Activities 2

  • This is a category under which things that are not placed in any of the above may be placed
  • Some clubs and hobby groups may be made for each of the above categories
  • so that students may pursue them even after SIP
