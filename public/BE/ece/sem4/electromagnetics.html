Electromagnetics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Electromagnetics (subject code BE04000301) as prescribed for ECE · Semester 4 divides the course into 10 units, covering 69 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE04000301)

Unit 1 Vector Analysis

5 lecture hours

  • Scalars and Vectors
  • Vector Algebra
  • The Rectangular Coordinate System
  • Vector Components and Unit Vectors
  • The Vector Field
  • The Dot Product
  • The Cross Product
  • Other Coordinate Systems: Circular Cylindrical Coordinate System
  • Spherical Coordinate System
  • Del Operator
  • Curl and Laplacian

Unit 2 Coulomb’s Law and Electric Field Intensity

4 lecture hours

  • The Experimental Law of Coulomb
  • Electric Field Intensity
  • Field Arising from a Continuous Volume Charge Distribution
  • Field of a Line Charge
  • Field of a Sheet of Charge
  • Streamlines and Sketches of Fields

Unit 3 Electric Flux Density, Gauss’s Law and Divergence

4 lecture hours

  • Electric Flux Density
  • Gauss’s Law and Application of Gauss’s Law: Some Symmetrical Charge Distributions and Differential Volume Element
  • Divergence and Maxwell’s First Equation
  • The Vector Operator ∇ and the Divergence Theorem

Unit 4 Energy and Potential

4 lecture hours

  • Energy Expended in Moving a Point Charge in an Electric Field
  • The Line Integral
  • Definition of Potential Difference and Potential
  • The Potential Field of a Point Charge
  • The Potential Field of a System of Charges: Conservative Property
  • Potential Gradient
  • The Electric Dipole
  • Energy Density in the Electrostatic Field

Unit 5 Conductors and Dielectrics

4 lecture hours

  • Current and Current Density
  • Continuity of Current
  • Metallic Conductors
  • Conductor Properties and Boundary Conditions
  • The Method of Images
  • The Nature of Dielectric Materials
  • Boundary Conditions for Perfect Dielectric Materials

Unit 6 Capacitance

4 lecture hours

  • Parallel-Plate Capacitor
  • Several Capacitance Examples
  • Capacitance of a Two-Wire Line
  • Using Field Sketches to Estimate Capacitance in Two-Dimensional Problems
  • Poisson’s and Laplace’s Equations
  • Examples of the Solution of Laplace’s Equation
  • Example of the Solution of Poisson’s Equation: the p-n Junction Capacitance Program Name: Bachelor of Engineering Level: UG Subject Code: BE04000301 Subject Name : Electromagnetics

Unit 7 The Steady Magnetic Field

4 lecture hours

  • Bio-Savart Law, Ampere’s Circuital Law, Curl
  • Stokes’ Theorem
  • Magnetic Flux and Magnetic Flux Density
  • The Scalar and Vector Magnetic Potentials
  • Derivation of the Steady-Magnetic-Field Laws

Unit 8 Magnetic Forces, Materials and Inductance

4 lecture hours

  • Force on a Moving Charge
  • Force on a Differential Current Element
  • Hall Effect
  • Force between Differential Current Elements
  • Force and Torque on a Closed Circuit
  • The Nature of Magnetic Materials
  • Magnetization and Permeability
  • Magnetic Boundary Conditions
  • The Magnetic Circuit
  • Potential Energy and Forces on Magnetic Materials

Unit 9 Time-Varying Fields and Maxwell’s Equations

4 lecture hours

  • Faraday’s Law, Displacement Current
  • Maxwell’s Equations in Point Form
  • Maxwell’s Equations in Integral Form
  • The Retarded Potentials

Unit 10 Electromagnetic Wave Propagation

5 lecture hours

  • Wave Propagation in Free Space
  • Lossy and Lossless Dielectrics and in Good Conductors
  • Reflection of Plane Wave
  • Poynting Vector
  • Wave Power
  • Skin Effect
  • Wave Polarization and Standing Wave Ratio
