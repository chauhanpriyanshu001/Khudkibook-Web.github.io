Study Free. Study Smart. Study Khudkibook.

Khudkibook is a free, student-first platform built for engineering students studying under Gujarat Technological University (GTU) — covering both Diploma and BE Degree programmes. Every textbook, syllabus, and previous year paper on this site is free to access, forever.

Our Mission

Students across Gujarat are often told that good study material is expensive or scattered across a dozen websites. Before exams, finding the right textbook in the right language becomes a race against time. Khudkibook solves this by bringing textbooks, official syllabi, and solved previous year papers for every branch and semester onto one fast, mobile-friendly platform.

We focus on two things above all: English and Gujarati medium coverage (so students can study in the language they are most comfortable in) and updated previous year papers so students always practise with recent, relevant exam patterns.

Why Students Choose Us

  • 100% free downloads. No payments, no OTP tricks, no "premium" lockouts — ever.
  • Complete branch coverage. 40+ Diploma branches and 100+ BE branches, organised semester by semester.
  • English & Gujarati books. Chapter-wise PDFs in both mediums where available.
  • Official syllabus linked. Accurate, GTU-official curriculum so you always study the right topics.
  • question papers updated every session. Winter and Summer papers added after each exam season.
  • A mobile app too. Read offline and download with one tap on Android.
