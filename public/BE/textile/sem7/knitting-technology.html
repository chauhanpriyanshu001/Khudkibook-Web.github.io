Knitting Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Knitting Technology (subject code 3172911) as prescribed for Textile Tech. · Semester 7 divides the course into 12 units, covering 54 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3172911)

Unit 1 Introduction to knitting, Knit structure- its unique features and applications,

4 lecture hours

  • comparison of knitting with weaving with respect to production and properties
  • Different knitting terminologies
  • Representation of a knit structure
  • laddering in a knit structure

Unit 2 Basic knitting elements of weft knitting machine (Needle bed, Sinker, Cam, Needles),

  • Different types of needles used in knitting
  • Knitting actions of all needles
  • Comparison of all needles

Unit 3 Circular weft knitting

  • Introduction to Weft Knitting
  • Passage of yarn through different parts of circular weft knitting machine
  • Study of elements of knitting machines such as: creel
  • yarn feeding
  • loop forming mechanism
  • take down motion
  • stop motions etc
  • Knitting cycle and basic elements of knitting
  • Essential elements of knitting machine – yarn supply arrangement
  • loop forming arrangement and fabric take down mechanism

Unit 5 Different types of principal stitches such as knit, miss, held loop, drop or press-off

3 lecture hours

  • float stitch
  • tuck stitch and their representation and their influence on fabric properties
  • Structural modifications commonly used in weft knitting
  • laying in, plating
  • open work structure

Unit 6 Recent developments in knitting processes such as Relative Technology (Relanit) for

  • circular knitting machines
  • knitting of fleecy and plush fabrics
  • striper and loop transfer mechanism. 2 Subject Code: 3172911 w.e.f

Unit 7 Types of defects and their remedial measures for weft knitted fabrics, Requirements

2 lecture hours

  • for yarn quality
  • GSM and production calculations

Unit 8 Flat knitting

3 lecture hours

  • Basic elements and their functions
  • Different types of machines and their operations for various stitches like miss
  • transfer and drop stitch
  • Socks and Gloves Knitting: Different types of machines with their principles of working used for above mentioned items

Unit 9 Warp Knitting

2 lecture hours

  • Introduction to Warp Knitting
  • comparison of weft and warp knitting
  • warp knit structure
  • application of warp knit structures
  • loop formation in a warp knit structure
  • Warp preparation for warp knitting

Unit 10 Warp knitting machine Raschel and Tricot

6 lecture hours

  • Passage of yarn through different parts of warp knitting machine
  • Elements of warp knitting machine (Needle
  • sinker and guide bar)
  • Swinging and shogging motion of guide bar, Sequence of loop formation in Raschel and Tricot warp knitting machine
  • Warp knit fabric representation
  • lapping plan and diagram

Unit 11 Studies of different warp knit structures like

6 lecture hours

  • Structures and properties of Single guide bar construction (Pillar stitch
  • 1 X 1, 2 X 1 and other Tricot lap open/ close loop
  • Atlas construction)
  • Structures and properties of two guide bar construction (Two bar tricot
  • Reverse lockint
  • Raised loop
  • Net structures
  • Double needle bed warp knit- pile fabric

Unit 12 Types of defects and their remedial measures for warp knitted fabrics, Requirements

3 lecture hours

  • for yarn quality
  • GSM and production calculations
  • Modern developments – whole garment production
