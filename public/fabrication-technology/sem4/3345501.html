Instrumentation Control & Quality Assurance Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Instrumentation Control & Quality Assurance (subject code 3345501) as prescribed for FT · Semester 4 divides the course into 8 units, covering 80 listed topics.
