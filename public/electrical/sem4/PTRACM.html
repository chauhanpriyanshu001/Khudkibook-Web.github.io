Polyphase Transformers and Rotating AC Machines Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Polyphase Transformers and Rotating AC Machines (subject code 4340901) as prescribed for ELECTRICAL · Semester 4 divides the course into 4 units, covering 38 listed topics. This curriculum applies from the March-2023 intake. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Three Phase Transformer

  • 1.1. Comparison of three phase transformer with bank of three single phase transformer.
  • 1.2. Construction of three phase transformer (Core and winding arrangement, Types of Winding.)
  • 1.3. Winding Connections / Vector group of three phase transformers. (Star- Star, Delta-Delta, Delta-Star, Star- Polyphase Transformers and Rotating AC Machines Course Code: 4340901 Topics and Sub-topics the 3-phase transformer.
  • 1.4. Parallel Operation of three phase Transformers.
  • 1.5. OFF load and ON load tap changer.
  • 1.6. Accessories of three phase transformers, Buchholz relay, Name plate of three phase transformers.
  • 1.7. Cooling of transformers, Natural and forced cooling.

Unit 2 Three phase Induction Motors

  • 2.1. Construction of a three phase induction motor and its types and applications.
  • 2.2. Rotating magnetic field due to two phase supply and three phase supply.
  • 2.3. Working Principle of three phase induction motor, Synchronous speed and Slip.
  • 2.4. Effect of slip in rotor circuit parameters.
  • 2.5. Derivation of Starting and Running torque, condition for maximum torque, Relation between torque and maximum torque.
  • 2.6. Torque slip and torque speed curve of three phase induction motor. (Generating, breaking and motoring mode)
  • 2.7. Power stages in an induction motor.
  • 2.8. Equivalent circuit of 3 phase I.M.
  • 2.9. Induction generator and its application
  • 2.10. Starting of three phase I.M. Necessity and types of starters- DOL, Star delta.
  • 2.11. Speed control of squirrel cage and slip- ring induction motor.
  • 2.12. No Load, Block rotor test and Circle diagram. Polyphase Transformers and Rotating AC Machines Course Code: 4340901 Topics and Sub-topics

Unit 3 Single Phase Induction motor 3a.Discuss two field revolving theory in single phase induction motor. 3b.Describe the working principle of...

  • 3.1. Double field Revolving Theory.
  • 3.2. Making Single phase induction motor self-starting.
  • 3.3. Types of Single phase induction motor. Split phase induction motor, Shaded pole motor, Resistance start motor, Capacitor start motor, Capacitor start capacitor run motor.
  • 3.4. Equivalent circuit of Single Phase induction motor.

Unit 4 Synchronous machines

  • 4.1. Construction of Alternator and its types.
  • 4.2. Alternator Operation, Frequency
  • 4.3. A.C. Armature windings, Winding Factors, Numerical
  • 4.4. EMF Equation of Alternator.
  • 4.5. Equivalent Circuit and Phasor Diagram of Alternator.
  • 4.6. Armature Reaction and its effect.
  • 4.7. Voltage Regulation of Alternator Determination of voltage regulation by direct loading method and Synchronous impedance method.
  • 4.8. Parallel Operation of alternator
  • 4.9. Synchronization of alternator with infinite bus bar/ alternator.
  • 4.10. Cooling of an alternator, Applications of alternators.
  • 4.11. Construction and working of synchronous motor and its starting.
  • 4.12. Effect of change in Excitation, V curve of Synchronous motor.
  • 4.13. Hunting and its prevention.
  • 4.14. Synchronous condenser and power factor improvement.
  • 4.15. Applications of Synchronous motors and its comparison with induction motor. Polyphase Transformers and Rotating AC Machines Course Code: 4340901
