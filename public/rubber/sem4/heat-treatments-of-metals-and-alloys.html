Heat Treatments Of Metals & Alloys Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Heat Treatments Of Metals & Alloys (subject code 3342105) as prescribed for Rubber · Semester 4 divides the course into 7 units, covering 34 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3342105)

Unit 1 Pyrometry

  • 1.1. Importance & Relevance
  • 1.2. Resistance pyrometers: principles, construction and working.
  • 1.3. Thermocouples pyrometry: principles, construction and working.
  • 1.4. Radiation pyrometry: principles, construction and working.
  • 1.5. Optical pyrometers: principles, construction and working.

Unit 3 Normalising

  • 3.1. Principle, objectives & process of Normalizing.
  • 3.2. Difference between Annealing & normalizing.

Unit 5 T.T.T. Diagram

  • 5.1. Definition & its importance.
  • 5.3. T.T.T. diagram for different types of steel.
  • 5.4. Factors affecting T.T.T. diagram & its limitations.

Unit 6 Surface Hardening processes

  • 6.1. Objectives of surface Hardening.
  • 6.5. Induction Hardening & Flame Hardening.
