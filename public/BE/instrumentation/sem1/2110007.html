Environmental Studies Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Environmental Studies (subject code 2110007) as prescribed for IC · Semester 1 divides the course into 4 units, covering 102 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

  • Effects of water pollutants
  • Eutrophication b) Marine pollution- c) Air Pollution: Composition of air, Structure of atmosphere
  • Ambient Air Quality Standards
  • Classification of air pollutants
  • Sources of common air pollutants like PM, SO2, NOX, Natural & Anthropogenic Sources
  • Effects of common air pollutants d) Land Pollution: Land uses
  • Land degradation: causes
  • soil erosion e) Noise Pollution: Introduction
  • Sound and Noise
  • Noise measurements
  • Causes and Effects f) Thermal Pollution: Causes and effects g) Role of individual in the prevention of pollution

  • Global Warming and Green House Effect

  • Depletion of Ozone layer
