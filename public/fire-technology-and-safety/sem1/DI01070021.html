First Aid & Home Nursing Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for First Aid & Home Nursing (subject code DI01070021) as prescribed for FTAS · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 8 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 FUNDAMENTALS OF FIRST AID AND BASIC LIFE

9 lecture hours

  • SUPPORT 1.1 Introduction to First Aid  Definition and objectives of first aid  Importance and scope of first aid  Principles of first aid  Responsibilities and limitations of a first aider  Assessment of an emergency situation  Personal safety and use of PPE  Emergency communication and calling for professional help 1.2 Basic Life Support  Assessment of an unconscious casualty  Checking responsiveness and breathing  Recovery position  Basic principles of CPR  Adult CPR  Introduction to child and infant CPR  Use of CPR face shield and barrier devices  Management of choking 1.3 Artificial Respiration  Basic principles of artificial respiration  Mouth-to-mouth respiration
