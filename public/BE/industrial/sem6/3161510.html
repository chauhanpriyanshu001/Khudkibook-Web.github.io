The official GTU curriculum for Quality and Reliability Engineering (subject code 3161510) as prescribed for Industrial · Semester 6 divides the course into 4 units, covering 36 listed topics. 8 lecture hours

  • 7QC Tools

  • 7 New Quality Improvement Tools
  • TQM Implementation and Limitations

  • Achieving robust design
