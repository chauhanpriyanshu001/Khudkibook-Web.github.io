Computational Method for Mechanical Engineering Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Computational Method for Mechanical Engineering (subject code 2710002) as prescribed for M(E · Semester 1 divides the course into 10 units, covering 153 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

6 lecture hours

  • Basic Concepts: Modelling
