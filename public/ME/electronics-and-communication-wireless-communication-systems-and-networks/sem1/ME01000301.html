Wireless and Mobile Communication Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Wireless and Mobile Communication (subject code ME01000301) as prescribed for EAC( · Semester 1 divides the course into 5 units, covering 49 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01000301)

Unit 1 Cellular Communication Fundamentals

9 lecture hours

  • Cellular system design
  • Frequency reuse
  • cell splitting
  • handover concepts
  • Co channel and adjacent channel interference
  • interference reduction techniques and methods to improve cell coverage
  • Frequency management and channel assignment
  • GSM architecture and interfaces
  • GSM architecture details
  • GSM subsystems
  • GSM Logical Channels
  • Data Encryption in GSM, Mobility Management, and Call Flows in GSM.2.5 G Standards: High speed Circuit Switched Data (HSCSD)
  • General Packet Radio Service (GPRS)
  • 2.75 G Standards: EDGE

Unit 2 Multiple Access Techniques

9 lecture hours

  • Spectral efficiency analysis based on calculations for Multiple access technologies: TDMA
  • CDMA and SDMA
  • Comparison of these technologies based on their signal separation techniques
  • disadvantages and application areas
  • Wireless network planning (Link budget and power spectrum calculations)

Unit 3 Mobile Radio Propagation

9 lecture hours

  • Large Scale Path Loss
  • Free Space Propagation Model
  • Ground Reflection (Two-Ray) Model
  • Practical Link Budget Design using Path Loss Models
  • Outdoor Propagation Models
  • Indoor Propagation Models
  • Signal Penetration into Buildings
  • Small Scale Fading and Multipath Propagation
  • Impulse Response Model
  • Multipath Measurements
  • Parameters of Multipath channels
  • Types of Small Scale Fading: Time Delay Spread
  • Frequency selective
  • Doppler Spread
  • Fast and Slow fading

Unit 4 Higher Generation Cellular Standards

9 lecture hours

  • 3G Standards: evolved EDGE
  • enhancements in 4G standard
  • Architecture and representative protocols
  • OFDM in LTE, SC-FDMA
  • Channel structure of LTE, Call flow for LTE, VoLTE

Unit 5 5G Communications

9 lecture hours

  • 5G wireless channels
  • Channel models for mmWave MIMO
  • Transmission and design of waveforms for 5G, OFDM
  • GFDMA and NOMA
  • Device to device and Machine to machine communications
  • millimeter wave communications
  • beam forming
  • Physical layer techniques
  • Massive MIMO-propagation models and channel estimation
