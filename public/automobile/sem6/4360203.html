Maintenance & Service -III Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Maintenance & Service -III (subject code 4360203) as prescribed for Auto · Semester 6 divides the course into 4 units, covering 23 listed topics. This curriculum applies from the Jan-2024 intake. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Automotive Battery 1.a Implementing battery safety and performance. 1.b Evaluating battery-related problems and solutions. 1.c Apply diff...

  • 1.1. Battery Maintenance and safety precaution: - Precaution steps taken while servicing battery. - Visually inspect for damage or corrosion. - Cleaning of battery terminals and connectors. - Topping up electrolyte (for lead acid battery)
  • 1.2. Battery diagnosis and troubleshooting: - Identifying common battery- related issues. - Symptoms of a weak or faulty battery. - Diagnosing battery drain or parasitic draw. - Different trouble causes and remedies of the battery.
  • 1.3. Battery Testing: - Various terminology used for battery testing. - Voltage measurement using a multimeter, Load test, Hydrometer test, Cadmium test. - Testing via battery analyzer. - Interpreting battery test results.
  • 1.4. Battery Charging and jump start: - Various charging processes and their use. - Step-by-step process of connecting jumper cables correctly and safely. Unit II 2.a Apply safe and effective maintenance to the ignition system.
  • 2.1. Maintenance of ignition system: - Periodic maintenance of ignition system. (Spark plugs, ignition Maintenance & Service –III Course Code: 4360203 Automotive Ignition system 2.b Applying precise troubleshooting and diagnostic techniques for ignition faults. 2.c Analyzing, testing, and interpreting ignition system components. coil, control module, wiring system, triggering devices) - Safety precaution during Maintenance and service of ignition system.
  • 2.2. Diagnosis of ignition system: - Diagnostic Procedures for Identifying Ignition System Faults. - Troubleshooting Common Ignition System Issues and their remedies.
  • 2.3. Testing of ignition systems: - Checking and setting ignition timing. - Testing of ignition system using oscilloscope. - Testing of all components of ignition system. - Testing of spark plug. - Interpreting various test results.
  • 2.4. Recycling and disposal of Ignition system.

Unit 2 Automotive Starting system 3.a Apply safe and effective maintenance to the Starting system. 3.b Applying precise troubleshooting and

  • 3.1. Maintenance of Starting system. - Safety precaution during Maintenance and service of Starting system.
  • 3.2. Diagnosis of Starting system - Diagnostic Procedures for Identifying Starting System Faults. - Troubleshooting Procedure and starting system problem with remedies and action plan.
  • 3.3. Testing of Starting system - Visual inspection test. - Control circuit test. - Starter circuit voltage drop test. - Starter amperage test. - Starter motor field coil, growler armature and runout test.
  • 3.4. Overhauling of starting system. Maintenance & Service –III Course Code: 4360203 - Disassembly and assembly with precise safety of starting system components. - Proper inspection and testing of each component i.e. solenoid, drive clearance, brush inspection, bench test.
  • 3.5. Recycling and disposal of starting system.

Unit 3 Automotive Charging system 4.a Apply safe and effective maintenance to the Charging system. 4.b Applying precise troubleshooting and

  • 4.1. Maintenance of Charging system - Safety precaution during Maintenance and service of charging system.
  • 4.2. Diagnosis of charging system. - Diagnostic Procedures for Identifying Charging System Faults. - Troubleshooting Procedure and charging system problem with remedies and action plan.
  • 4.3. Testing of Charging system - Charging voltage test. - Drive belt inspection and adjustment. - AC ripple voltage & current test. - Voltage drop test. - Alternator output test
  • 4.4. Overhauling of Charging system - Disassembly and assembly with precise safety of charging system components. - Various test during overhauling i.e. testing of an alternator, rotor, stator, diode, and rectifier.
  • 4.5. Recycling and disposal of Charging system.

  • 5.2. Evolution of On-Board Diagnosis (OBD)
  • 5.3. Importance of Diagnosis Trouble Codes (DTCs) in Vehicle Diagnostics.
