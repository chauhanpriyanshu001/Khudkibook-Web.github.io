6 lecture hours

  • Overview of Digital Marketing: Traditional vs Digital Marketing Key Differences

  • Organic vs Paid Digital Marketing
  • AI Tools in Digital Marketing: ChatGPT
  • Google Gemini

  • Simple payment tools: Payment gateways (e.g.

  • Case Studies: How small businesses (e.g.
  • a local tech startup) use e-commerce platforms?

Unit 3 Search & Display Marketing

11 lecture hours

  • Search Engine Basics: How search engines like Google work
  • ranking of websites

  • Simple SEO techniques Online Ads (Google Ads): Creating simple ads, Measuring ad success: Basic metrics like clicks and views
  • Case studies: Simple SEO and ads (e.g.
  • how a local store uses Google)
  • Emerging Trends: New tools like voice search (e.g.
  • asking Google Home about products)
