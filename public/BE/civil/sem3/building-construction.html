Building Construction Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Building Construction (subject code 2130607) as prescribed for Civil Engineering · Semester 3 divides the course into 6 units, covering 87 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

  • types – brick arches

  • flat – semi circular. 5 Plain and Reinforced Concrete Construction: Pre-cast and cast-in-situ Construction

  • types of flat terraced roofing. 8 Wall Finishes: Plastering
  • pointing and painting 9 Temporary Works : Timbering in trenches
  • types of scaffoldings
