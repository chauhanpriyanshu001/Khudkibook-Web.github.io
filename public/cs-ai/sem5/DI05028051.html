Management of Textile Processing Industries Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Management of Textile Processing Industries (subject code DI05028051) as prescribed for CS-AI · Semester 5 divides the course into 5 units, covering 6 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (DI05028051)

Unit 1 Management in Textile Wet Processing Industry

8 lecture hours

  • 1.1 Objectives and functions of industrial management 1.2 Classification of Management 1.3 Scientific and traditional management and their impact 1.4 Features of textile processing industry management 1.5 Plant layout types and site selection in wet processing industry

Unit 2 Production Planning & Control

12 lecture hours

  • 2.1 Production management and methods of production 2.2 Production planning and control (PPC) 2.3 Role of supervisor in textile processing units 2.4 Principles of material handling and flow patterns 2.5 Function and Types of material handling 2.6 Phenomenon to achieve Right First Time 2.7 Concept of Inventory Management

Unit 3 Cost Control & Cost Accounting

8 lecture hours

  • 3.1 Elements of cost and cost control techniques 3.2 Theory of costing 3.3 Profit vs profitability 3.4 Break-even analysis 3.5 Depreciation and obsolescence

Unit 4 Sales and Marketing Management

10 lecture hours

  • 4.1 Sales and marketing management 4.2 Organization and functions of sales department 4.3 Marketing strategies and marketing functions 4.4 Role of Marketing
  • Sales and Merchandising Manager 4.5 Difference between marketing and merchandising 4.6 Advertising: methods and media 4.7 Impact of marketing and advertising on sales

Unit 5 Industrial Relations, Safety and Environmental Laws

7 lecture hours

  • 4.1 Indian Factory Act (objectives and importance) 4.2 Labour welfare acts and social accountability (SA 8000) 4.3 Boiler Act 4.4 Pollution control acts 4.5 Environmental laws and sustainable practices in textile industry 4.6 Functions of Training Department
