Rationale:
The aim of introducing this course on digital electronics to impart knowledge
of basic building blocks of digital logic circuits. This will enable the student to
become aware of various number systems, logic gates and logic families,
combinational and sequential logic circuits, which is the foundation for
understanding the digital controls, microprocessors and computer systems.
Through this course the student will be able to apply the same in almost all
areas of electronic control and develop the testing skills.
Course Outcome:
After Completion of the Course, Student will able to:
