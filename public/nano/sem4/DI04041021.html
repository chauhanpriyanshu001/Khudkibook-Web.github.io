Process Measurement Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Process Measurement (subject code DI04041021) as prescribed for Nano · Semester 4 divides the course into 4 units, covering 101 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Temperature Measurement Techniques

  • 1.1. Introduction to Temperature Measurement
  • 1.1.1. Temperature units and scales: Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Kelvin (K)
  • 1.1.2. Industrial temperature ranges
  • 1.2.1. Seebeck effect (Thermoelectric principle)
  • 1.2.2. Thermoelectric laws (Law of intermediate metals & temperatures)
  • 1.2.3. Modern thermocouple types (J, K, R, S, T, N)
  • 1.2.4. Cold junction compensation (CJC methods)
  • 1.2.5. Thermocouple temperature calculation (mV- Temperature conversion using ITS-90 tables)
  • 1.3. Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTD)
  • 1.3.1. Industrial RTD construction (Pt100, Class A/B)
  • 1.3.2. Lead wire compensation – need & principle
  • 1.3.3. Measuring circuits (2-wire, 3-wire, 4-wire)
  • 1.4.1. NTC thermistor
  • 1.4.2. PTC thermistor
  • 1.4.3. Resistance-Temperature (R-T) characteristics
  • 1.5. Integrated Circuit (IC) Temperature Sensors
