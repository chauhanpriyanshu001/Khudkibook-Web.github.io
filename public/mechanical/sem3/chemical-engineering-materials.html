Chemical Engineering Materials Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Chemical Engineering Materials (subject code 3330501) as prescribed for MECHANICAL · Semester 3 divides the course into 7 units, covering 54 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3330501)

Unit 1 Properties Of Materials Describe scope of material science Explain important properties of materials

  • 1.1. Scope of material science
  • 1.2. Definition and explanation of : Melting point, Boiling point, Specific heat, Thermal conductivity, Thermal expansion, Thermal insulation, Stresses, Strain, Yield stress, Fatigue,Creep
  • 1.3. Principles of selection of materials

Unit 2 Corrosion and Its Prevention Define corrosion and describe it’s types Control and prevent corrosion

  • 2. 1 Definition of corrosion
  • 2. 2 Types of corrosion: Direct corrosion, Electro-chemical corrosion, Galvanic corrosion, High temperature corrosion
  • 2. 3 Factors affecting corrosion rate
  • 2. 4 Methods for control and prevention of corrosion

Unit 3 Metals and Alloys Describe and compare ferrous metals and alloys Describe non-Ferrous

  • 3. 1 Properties and uses of Cast iron, Wrought iron, Mild steel, Stainless steel
  • 3. 2 Comparison of ferrous metals and alloys
  • 3. 3 Properties and uses of metals: Aluminium, Zinc, Chromium, Nickel, Tin, Copper, Titanium, Tungsten, Platinum and Silver
  • 3. 4 Properties and uses of alloys : Duralumin, Brass, Bronze, Inconel, Hastalloy B and C, Invar, Chemical Engineering Materials Course code: 3330501 GTU/NITTTR/Bhopal/12-13 Gujarat State 3 Unit Major Learning Outcomes (Course Outcomes in Cognitive Domain according to NBA terminology) Topics and Sub-topics Y alloy
  • 3. 5 Purification of metals using Blast furnace and Arc furnace

Unit 4 Ceramic Materials

  • 4.1. Ceramic materials
  • 4.2. Composition, properties and uses of china clay, fire clay, bentonite
  • 4.3. Classification, properties and uses of refractories
  • 4.4. Composition, properties and uses of Soda lime glass, borosilicate glass, high silica glass, fibre glass, glass wool, form glass
  • 4.5. Composition, properties and uses of Porcelain

Unit 5 Organic Materials

  • 5.1. Definition and importance of Polymer
  • 5.2. Addition and condensation Polymerization
  • 5.3. Plastics : definition, classification, general properties and uses
  • 5.4. Rubbers : definition, classification, general properties and uses
  • 5.5. Compare natural and synthetic rubber
  • 5.6. Vulcanizing of rubber Chemical Engineering Materials Course code: 3330501 GTU/NITTTR/Bhopal/12-13 Gujarat State 4 Unit Major Learning Outcomes (Course Outcomes in Cognitive Domain according to NBA terminology) Topics and Sub-topics

Unit 6 Protective Coatings and Insulations

  • 6.1. Paints: classification and uses
  • 6.2. Ingredients of paints: their properties and importance
  • 6.3. Special types of paints and their applications
  • 6.4. Varnishes: classification and uses
  • 6.5. Ingredients of Varnishes
  • 6.6. Types of insulations
  • 6.7. Properties and applications of different :(i) Electric insulation (ii)Thermal insulation

Unit 7 Composites, Lubricants and Adhesives

  • 8.1. List of composite materials
  • 8.2. Properties and uses of Fiber reinforced plastics(FRP), Metal matrix composites(MMC), Ceramic matrix composites(CMC)
  • 8.3. Classification, properties and uses of Synthetic lubricants, Semisolid lubricants
  • 8.4. Adhesives: classification, properties and uses Chemical Engineering Materials Course code: 3330501 GTU/NITTTR/Bhopal/12-13 Gujarat State 5
