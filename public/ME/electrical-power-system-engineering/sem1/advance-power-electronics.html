Advance Power Electronics Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Advance Power Electronics (subject code ME01000141) as prescribed for E(SE · Semester 1 divides the course into 7 units, covering 55 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01000141)

Unit 1 Review of Semiconductor Power Devices

6 lecture hours

  • Review of Semiconductor devices like Power diode
  • Power BJT, SCR, MOSFET
  • IGBT, GTO, MCT
  • Static and dynamic characteristics of these devices
  • controlled and uncontrolled switches
  • Single quadrant
  • Two quadrant and bi-directional switches

Unit 2 Switching Voltage Regulators

8 lecture hours

  • Linear power supply (voltage regulators)
  • Switching voltage regulators
  • Review of basic dc-dc voltage regulator configurations -Buck
  • Buck-Boost converters and their analysis for continuous and discontinuous mode
  • Other converter configurations like Flyback converter
  • Forward converter
  • Half bridge
  • Full bridge configurations
  • Push-pull converter
  • C’uk converter
  • Sepic Converter
  • Design criteria for SMPS
  • Multi-output switch mode regulator

Unit 3 Design of Magnetic Components

8 lecture hours

  • Design of power transformer
  • high frequency transformers for flyback
  • half-bridge–full bridge and push pull converters
  • Design of inductors for various converter topologies
  • Design of current transformers for high frequency current measurement
  • Different types of core materials

Unit 4 DC-AC converters / Inverters

6 lecture hours

  • Review of line commutated inverters
  • Bridge inverters with 120°
  • 180°, and 150° modes of operation
  • Harmonic reduction techniques
  • Sine-triangular PWM
  • Space Vector Pulse Width Modulation
  • Current Source Inverters

Unit 5 Gate and Base drive circuits

7 lecture hours

  • Preliminary design considerations
  • DC coupled drive circuits with unipolar and bipolar outputs
  • Importance of isolation in driver circuits
  • Electrically isolated drive circuits
  • Some commonly available driver chips (based on boot-strap capacitor)
  • Cascade connected drive circuits
  • Thyristor drive circuits
  • Protection in driver circuits
  • Blanking circuits for bridge inverters

Unit 6 DC-AC and AC-AC converters

7 lecture hours

  • Review of On-off and phase control
  • Three phase half-wave and full wave controllers and their analysis with resistive loads
  • circulating current operation
  • non-circulating current operation
  • mean output voltage and harmonics in supply current waveform
  • Multilevel converters
  • control and applications
  • Matrix converters
  • Three phase Cyclo-converters their working
  • Program Name: Engineering Level: PG Branch: Electrical Engineering Course / Subject Code: ME01000141 Course / Subject Name: Advance Power Electronics

Unit 7 Advancements

3 lecture hours

  • Latest developments in Power Electronics field related to new materials
  • power topologies
  • control strategies and ICs, applications etc
