Modern Weaving Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Modern Weaving Technology (subject code 3362902) as prescribed for ICT · Semester 6 divides the course into 5 units, covering 31 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (3362902)

Unit 1 Preparatory Processes for Shuttle Less Weaving

  • 1.1. Comparison between conventional and shuttle less looms.
  • 1.2. Shuttle less weaving machines: Types
  • 1.3. Modern developments and processing parameters for Quality Yarn preparation: Winding, Warping, Sizing
  • 1.4. Unconventional weaving machines: Weft accumulator, Weft measuring systems, Weft cutters

Unit 2 Weft Insertion by Projectile and Rapier

  • 2.1. Projectile weaving machine: features and use
  • 2.2. Weft insertion sequence in projectile weaving machine
  • 2.3. Principle of torsion picking mechanism,
  • 2.4. Beat up mechanism on Sulzer loom
  • 2.5. Selvedge formation in projectile looms
  • 2.6. Rapier weaving machines: Rigid, Flexible
  • 2.7. Weft insertion methods: Dewas system, Gabler system
  • 2.8. Rapier heads

Unit 3 Weft Insertion by Air Jet

  • 3.1. Essential Requirements of success full Air-jet
  • 3.2. Weft insertion.
  • 3.3. Types of Air guide: Insertion configurations,
  • 3.4. Confuser: Mono Nozzle, Multi Nozzle and Reed Profile
  • 3.5. Quality of Air: Methods of Air jet control / air distribution control
  • 3.6. Sequence of weft insertion in Air jet looms

Unit 4 Weft insertion by Water jet

  • 4.1. Air jet and water jet
  • 4.2. Water quality
  • 4.3. Weft supply system on Water jet loom
  • 4.4. Picking system of the Water jet loom
  • 4.5. Weft insertion in Water jet loom Modern Weaving Technology Course Code: 3362902 GTU/ NITTTR Bhopal/14 Gujarat State 3 Unit Major Learning Outcomes (In the Cognitive Domain) Topics and Sub-topics on Water Jet

Unit 5 Multiphase and Tri-axial Weaving and Un- Conventiona l Looms

  • 6.1. Multiphase weaving machine
  • 6.2. Warp way Multiphase weaving machine
  • 6.3. Weft way Multiphase weaving machine
  • 6.4. Triaxial weaving
  • 6.5. Quick style change (QSC) and Automation in: Drawing in, Variable Pick density, Variable speed, On loom inspection, On line loom monitoring system, Electronic shedding, Electronic take up and let off motion
  • 6.6. Automatic stop mark prevention
  • 6.7. Modifications in Weft Insertion system in AJ loom: Tapered Sub Nozzles, Tandom Nozzles, Tapered Tunnel Reed
  • 6.8. Automatic pick controller
