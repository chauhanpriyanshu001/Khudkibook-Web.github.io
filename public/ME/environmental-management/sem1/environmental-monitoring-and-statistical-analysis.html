Environmental Monitoring and Statistical Analysis Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Environmental Monitoring and Statistical Analysis (subject code ME01018041) as prescribed for EM · Semester 1 divides the course into 6 units, covering 48 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (ME01018041)

Unit 1 Environmental Monitoring

7 lecture hours

  • Purpose of monitoring
  • Scales of observation
  • Environmental characteristics
  • Representative units
  • Sampling Location
  • Types of environmental monitoring
  • Sampling plan
  • Analytical data quality requirements: Precision and Accuracy
  • Detection limits
  • Reporting data

Unit 2 Water Quality Monitoring

8 lecture hours

  • Sampling techniques
  • Preservation of water sample
  • Physical Properties of water & its monitoring: Temperature
  • Turbidity etc.
  • Chemical Properties of water & its monitoring 1
  • Electrometric method: pH 2.Colorimetric method 3.Spectroscopy method
  • Standardization & calibration of monitoring instruments

Unit 3 Physical, Chemical and Microbial contaminants

8 lecture hours

  • Physical contaminants – Naturally occurring particulates
  • Human made particulates
  • Mechanisms and control of particulate
  • Chemical contaminant:- Type of contaminants
  • Sources of Contaminants
  • contaminant transport and fate
  • Microbial contaminants:- Environmentally transmitted pathogens
  • concept of indicator organisms
  • sample processing and storage

Unit 4 Surface Water and Ground Water Monitoring

8 lecture hours

  • Surface Water Monitoring:-Water Quality parameters
  • sampling the waters
  • Water sampling equipments
  • Ground Water Monitoring: - Objectives
  • Location of monitor wells
  • well construction
  • Design and Execution of ground water sampling programs

Unit 5 Air Quality Monitoring

18 lecture hours

  • 08 GUJARATTECHNOLOGICALUNIVERSITY Program Name: Engineering Level:Post Graduate Branch: Environmental Management Course/Subject Code: ME01018041 Course / Subject Name: Environmental Monitoring and Statistical Analysis Type of Air Quality monitoring - Ambient Air Quality monitoring
  • Source Air Quality monitoring
  • Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Selection of monitoring sites
  • Sampling time
  • Frequency & mode of sampling
  • Source Air Quality Monitoring – Type of Monitoring procedure

Unit 6 Statistical analysis

6 lecture hours

  • Samples & Population : Random Sampling
  • Sample support
  • Frequency Distribution & Probability Density Function : Mean
  • Standard Deviation
  • Gaussian Variable
  • Sample size & Confidence interval
  • Covariance & Correlation
  • Liner Regression
  • Interpolation & Spatial Distribution
