Petroleum Refining & Petrochemicals Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Petroleum Refining & Petrochemicals (subject code 2180502) as prescribed for Chemical · Semester 8 divides the course into 2 units, covering 14 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (2180502)

Unit 1 Basic of Petroleum

6 lecture hours

  • Role of Crude oil in global economy
  • Present Scenario of Crude Oil Refinery
  • Classification and Evaluation of Crude oil, Crude Assay Analysis
  • Distillation Characteristics such as TBP, ASTM&EFV, etc

Unit 2 Properties of Crude and Petroleum Products

10 lecture hours

  • Various types of Average Boiling Points of Crude Oil & Petroleum Fractions.  Types of Gases & their Composition
  • Types of Gasoline & it’s Important Properties and tests such as ASTM Distillation, RVP, Octane Number
  • Oxidation Stability
  • Sulphur Content etc,  Various Types of Naphtha and their Important Properties & Applications.  Important Tests & Properties of Kerosene such as Flash& Fire Point
  • Smoke Point
  • Aniline Point etc.
  •  Types of Diesel & its Important Properties & Tests such as Pour Point
  • Diesel Index
  • Cetane Number etc.  Heavy Fractions like Lube Oil, Bitumen
  • Asphalt etc & their Important
