How To Check Your GTU Summer 2026 Result

Your scorecard is available from the official GTU portals. No third-party app is needed.

  1. GTU result portal — open gturesults.in, choose your examination and session, then enter your enrolment or seat number with the captcha.
  2. GTU student portal — log in at student.gtu.ac.in, open the Examination section and download your provisional marksheet.
  3. GTU website — go to gtu.ac.in → Student Corner → Examination → Result List and pick the link for your course.
Save a PDF of your marksheet as soon as it appears. College processes, re-check applications and scholarship forms all ask for a copy of the result document.

Understanding Your Marks: GTU Grade Points

GTU follows a 10-point grading system. SPI is your performance in one semester; CPI is the cumulative average across every semester you have completed.
