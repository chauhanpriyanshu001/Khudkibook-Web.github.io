How To Check Your GTU Summer 2026 Result

Your scorecard is available from the official GTU portals. No third-party app is needed.

  1. GTU result portal — open gturesults.in, choose your examination and session, then enter your enrolment or seat number with the captcha.
  2. GTU student portal — log in at student.gtu.ac.in, open the Examination section and download your provisional marksheet.
  3. GTU website — go to gtu.ac.in → Student Corner → Examination → Result List and pick the link for your course.
Save a PDF of your marksheet as soon as it appears. College processes, re-check applications and scholarship forms all ask for a copy of the result document.

Re-Check & Re-Assessment: How It Works

If a subject's marks look wrong, GTU lets you apply for a re-check (the answer sheet is totalled and verified again for any missed correction) or a re-assessment (a fresh examiner re-evaluates the paper).

ServiceStandard fee per subject
Re-checking₹100
Re-assessment₹250

Steps To Apply

  1. Log in to the GTU Student Portal with your enrolment number and password.
  2. Open the Examination section and choose Re-check / Re-assessment Application.
  3. Select the subjects, submit, and note the application number generated.
  4. Pay the fee through SBI Collect using that application number, and keep the receipt.
The re-check window is short and it is set by the Controller of Examination for each result batch — it is printed in the official notification PDF linked below. Missing it means waiting for the next cycle, so check the date immediately.
