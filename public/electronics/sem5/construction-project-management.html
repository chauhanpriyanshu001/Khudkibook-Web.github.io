Construction Project Management Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Construction Project Management (subject code DI05006031) as prescribed for Electronics · Semester 5 divides the course into 6 units, covering 59 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Unit 1 Construction Industry & Management

6 lecture hours

  • 1.1 Construction Project management- importance
  • Scope. 1.2 Organization-Types
  • principles. 1.3 Construction team-Roles
  • responsibilities and skills of construction team. 1.4 Stages in Construction. 1.5 Causes of Project failure

Unit 2 Tendering and Accounting

10 lecture hours

  • Contract documents and conditions of Contract
  • Contract agreement. 2.3
  • Per-qualification of Contract- Importance. 2.4
  • Terms and Conditions
  • issue procedure
  • Prepare tender Notice. 2.6
  • Technical terms- Administrative approval
  • Technical Sanction
  • Issue rate
  • Competent Authority
  • Secured Advance Mobilization Advance
  • Earnest money deposit (EMD) and Security deposit (SD) 2.7
  • Accounting terms- Work Abstract
  • Cash book
  • Work resister
  • accounting for the materials
  • Measurement book
  • Muster roll
  • types of bills and recording. 2.8
  • Methods of getting work done in government organization

Unit 3 Time Management Methods and Tools in Construction

13 lecture hours

  • Method of Construction Scheduling
  • Development of bar chart
  • Merits and limitations of bar chart. 3.2
  • Elements of Network: Event
  • dummy activities
  • Precautions in drawing Network
  • Numbering the events. 3.3
  • Work breakdown structure
  • activity cost and time estimation in CPM and PERT techniques
  • Type of Floats and their significance. 3.4
  • Critical path method-Important terms
  • Basic Rules
  • Advantages and disadvantages. 3.5
  • Examples of CPM and PERT network. 3.6
  • PERT analysis-Important terms
  • Advantages and disadvantages. 3.7
  • Cost optimization. 3.8 Introduction to Project Management software

Unit 4 Construction Resource Management

7 lecture hours

  • 4.1 Material management-Purpose
  • material Scheduling
  • material handling
  • safety precautions
  • Economy Order Quantity. 4.2 Job layout 4.3 Equipment Management: Equipment Scheduling
  • Factors affecting selection of equipment. 4.4 Various costs associated with equipment
  • Maintenance Management
  • Replacement of Equipment
  • Economic life of Equipment. 4.5 Manpower Management: Objectives
  • Labour schedule
  • output. 4.6 Suitable organization structure for construction industry. 4.7 Information Management: MIS – its concept and need

Unit 5 Human Resource development (HRD)& MIS

5 lecture hours

  • 5.1 Importance of HRD. 5.2 Supervisor’s role as trainer & Motivator. 5.3 Techniques to deal human resources effectively. 5.4 Professional Ethics in Engineering. 5.5 Management Information System: Purpose
  • Implementation and Applications

Unit 6 Safety Management

4 lecture hours

  • 6.1 Safety management-requirement
  • importance. 6.2 Causes of accidents and its type. 6.3 Safety precaution-Excavation work
  • Erection. 6.4 Safety measures- Scaffolding
  • Bituminous works
