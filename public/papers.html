The papers are linked straight from the university's own archive rather than re-hosted here, so what you open is the original.

Why Past Papers Are Worth More Than Notes

  • Why it matters 1

    A GTU paper tells you the real question pattern: how many long answers are expected, how the marks split between sections, and which units the examiner keeps returning to. No textbook states that as plainly.

  • Why it matters 2

    Attempting a paper under a two-hour clock is the only realistic way to find out whether you can actually write an answer in the time available. Reading a chapter feels like preparation; writing an answer under a clock is preparation.

  • Why it matters 3

    GTU repeats concepts across sittings more often than it repeats questions. Solving four or five years of papers exposes the concepts that keep coming back, which is close to a revision list.

  • Why it matters 4

    Papers also show you the format of marks allocation inside a question — how many marks for the derivation, how many for the diagram — which changes how much you should write.

How To Use The Selector

  1. 1

    Pick your branch from the first list. If you are unsure, match it against the branch printed on your hall ticket or timetable.

  2. 2

    Pick your semester from the second list. GTU semesters are numbered, so Sem-3 is the third semester of your programme, not the third year.

  3. 3

    You land on the semester's subject table. Open a subject and use the Paper button to see every previous year paper we have verified for it.

  4. 4

    Attempt the paper before you open the syllabus or the textbook. Then use the syllabus units on the same page to mark the gaps.

Questions GTU Students Ask

Is Khudkibook free to use?

Yes. Every syllabus, textbook link and previous year paper on this site is free, with no login wall and no payment step. The site is run by students, and its only aim is to keep GTU study material in one place that is easy to search.

Are the question papers official GTU papers?

The papers linked from subject pages come from Gujarat Technological University's own question-paper archive. We link to the source rather than hosting a copy, so what you open is the paper as the university published it.

Why does some material say Coming Soon?

We only publish a link once we have verified that the file exists and opens. When a subject has neither a textbook nor a paper on file yet, the page says Coming Soon instead of sending you to a dead link. Those subjects still carry the official syllabus PDF, which is enough to start.

Do you provide solutions or notes for the papers?

We publish the question papers themselves and the official syllabus. Treat the papers as your practice material: attempt them under a timer, then compare answers with your textbook. Group discussion with the same paper is usually more useful than a read-made solution PDF.

Which branches and semesters are covered?

The site covers GTU Diploma, BE Degree and ME programmes across every branch GTU offers, with a semester page for each one. Coverage depth varies by branch because paper availability varies; each semester page shows the exact subject count it currently holds.
