Chemical Process Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Chemical Process Technology (subject code BE03005021) as prescribed for Chemical · Semester 3 divides the course into 11 units, covering 62 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (BE03005021)

Unit 1 Chemical processing and the work of chemical engineering

2 lecture hours

  • Basic chemical data
  • unit operation and unit process
  • batch and continuous processing
  • process flow sheet
  • environment aspects
  • safety aspects etc

Unit 2 Sulphur, Sulphuric acid industry

5 lecture hours

  • Mining and manufacturing of Sulphur
  • synthesis of Sulphur dioxide
  • Manufacture of Sulphuric acid and its applications
  • manufacturing technologies & associated engineering problems

Unit 3 Fertilizer industry

5 lecture hours

  • manufacturing processes of Ammonia
  • Nitric acid
  • Phosphoric acid
  • their uses and applications
  • manufacturing of phosphate fertilizers
  • major engineering problems
  • NPK fertilizer

Unit 4 Chlor-alkali and heavy inorganic industry

4 lecture hours

  • Manufacturing of soda ash, Caustic Soda and Chlorine by membrane cell
  • mercury & diaphragm process

Unit 5 Dye & its intermediates, paints and pigment industry

5 lecture hours

  • Classification of dyes according to its constitution and application
  • Introduction to Disperse
  • Azo dyes
  • Koch acid
  • Vinyl sulphone
  • Vat dyes
  • Introduction to Paints classification & its constituents
  • PVC of Paints
  • different types of pigments such as white
  • blue, red, yellow
  • Introduction to Varnishes
  • Thinners & Industrial Coatings

Unit 6 Soap and detergent industry

4 lecture hours

  • Introduction to soap and detergent
  • soap manufacturing process
  • methods of detergent manufacture
  • manufacture of glycerine

Unit 7 Drugs and pharmaceuticals industry

4 lecture hours

  • Classification of various drugs and pharmaceuticals
  • Introduction of Antibiotics
  • Manufacturing of penicillin
  • Introduction of vitamins
  • Manufacturing processes of Aspirin
  • & C (Ascorbic acid)

Unit 8 Sugar & Fermentation Industry

4 lecture hours

  • Manufacturing of Sugar
  • Fermentation process
  • Industrial Alcohol
  • Absolute Alcohol
  • Wines and Liquors
  • Manufacturing of Butyl Alcohol & Citric acid by fermentation

Unit 9 Cement & ceramic industry

4 lecture hours

  • Cement & Its types
  • Limestone benefication
  • manufacturing of Portland cement
  • lime manufacture
  • Introduction to Ceramic Industry
  • basic raw materials and ceramic chemistry
  • manufacturing of Refractory bricks
  • Program Name: Bachelor of Engineering Level: UG Branch: Chemical Engineering Subject Code : BE03005021 Subject Name : Chemical Process Technology

Unit 10 Pulp and paper industry

4 lecture hours

  • Pulp manufacturing by Kraft process
  • major engineering problems
  • chemical recovery
  • Manufacturing of Paper

Unit 11 Industrial gases and carbon

4 lecture hours

  • Importance of cryogenics in gas production
  • production of gases like carbon dioxide
  • introduction to rare gases of atm. like helium and acetylene
