Elements of Textile and Garment Technology Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Elements of Textile and Garment Technology (subject code DI01028011) as prescribed for CS-AI · Semester 1 divides the course into 3 units, covering 11 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.

Download the official syllabus PDF (DI01028011)

Unit 1 Yarn Manufacturing Process and Yarn Numbering System

18 lecture hours

  • 1.1 Explain basic terminologies of textile. 1.2 Sequence of yarn formation. 1.3 Objects of various spinning process. 1.4 Passage of material through various spinning department machines with neat line diagram: (i) Modern Blow room Line (ii) Card (iii) Draw frame (iv) Lap former (v) Comber (vi) Speed frame (vii) Ring frame. 1.5 Yarn numbering system and its conversion (i) Indirect numbering system: English
  • Metric (ii) Direct numbering system: Tex, Denier

Unit 2 Yarn Texturizing and Manufacturing Process

18 lecture hours

  • 2.1 Texturizing process-Objectives
  • yarn texturizing method classification
  • the passage of filament through false twist and air jet texturizing machine. 2.2 Sequence of fabric manufacturing. 2.3 Objects of various weaving process. 2.4 Passage of material through various weaving department machines with neat line diagram: (i) Winding machine (ii) Warping machine (iii) Multi-cylinder sizing machine (iv) Plain Power loom. 2.5 Basic weaves -Plain
  • Sateen, and Satin with design
  • draft andpeg plan. 2.6 Knitting process- Introduction
  • Warp and Weft knitting principle

Unit 3 Introduction to Garment Technology

9 lecture hours

  • 3.1 Importance of garment industry to present textile trends. 3.2 Factor affecting selection of fabrics for final end product. 3.3 Stages of the garment manufacturing process. 3.4 Brief study of pattern design
  • grading system
  • spreading methods and types of cutter
