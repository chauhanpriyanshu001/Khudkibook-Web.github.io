Process Instrumentation-II Syllabus — Unit-wise Content

The official GTU curriculum for Process Instrumentation-II (subject code DI04017011) as prescribed for PE · Semester 4 divides the course into 4 units, covering 70 listed topics. The unit-wise breakdown below is read from the GTU syllabus document for this subject code, so you can check what the course actually covers before deciding which papers to attempt.
